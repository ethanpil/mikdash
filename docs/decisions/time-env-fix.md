# time-env-fix — decisions (review round on src/env)

Format: **choice** — why — alternative.

- **Sun aureole uses the disc's line-of-sight transmittance, slant coarse depth capped at 0.1, scaled by the disc's adaptation factor** — single forward scattering travels the same path as the direct beam, so aureole(1°)/disc stays ≈1e-4 (high Sun) … 1e-3 (horizon) as measured. Before, a 3 km "glow" transmittance (~10× too high) and an uncompressed aureole beside a disc capped at 3 units made the low Sun a 3–6° white blob (limb ≈ 600 output units, now ≈ 0.1) — compress the aureole only.
- **Disc cap = max(3 → ∞ by 25°, k × sky beside the Sun), k 2 at ≤1° → 5 at 4°; one global factor + a log shoulder** — the sky in the Sun's azimuth band is itself 4–9 output units at sunrise, so a fixed cap of 3 hid the disc even without the aureole. k = 2 keeps the most colour AgX allows (above ~8 the grade's inset matrix turns any red cream); by 3–4° the sky beside the Sun is near white, so the disc needs ~2 stops to read. The global factor (instead of a per-pixel clamp) keeps limb darkening and the redder, dimmer lower limb — a per-pixel clamp (flat disc) or a hue-preserving compression of the whole near-Sun sky (non-physical, a tone-mapping job).
- **Refraction flattening of the Sun's disc** from the gradient of Bennett's formula (0.78 on the horizon, 0.9 at 2.5°, 1 above 20°) — the low Sun is visibly oval; it is what an observer of netz sees over Har HaMishchah — a round disc.
- **No extra dawn-glow term** — with the false Sun removed, the sky-view LUT already holds a broad warm band centred on the Sun's azimuth that widens sideways along the horizon (Yoma 28b, "spreads this way and that"); any added lobe would be non-physical — a broad HG-in-azimuth horizon lobe (reviewer's option).
- **Night floor = N·(vanRhijn·T + 2·(1−T)) / zenith value**, and toward the ground N·2·(1−T_ground) (transmittance-ratio trick) — the old floor (vanRhijn·T only) went black over the last 4–7° and below the horizon; the in-scatter term gives 2.3× the zenith at 10° (≈0.9 mag brighter, Roach & Gordon / Garstang), 1.7× at the horizon, continuous across the dip horizon. The tint is normalised to luminance 1 so the dome zenith is exactly 2e-4 cd/m², the value exposure, fog, ambient and star limiting magnitude use; all of them now read the same curve (`nightFloorShape` in atmosphere.js) — fixed ×2.5 / ×1.8 CPU factors that disagreed with the dome. Side effect: the moonless dome is ~1.9× brighter than before (it was 0.52 N); it still reads as night (slate blue, darker ground, stars).
- **scene.fog.density = √(σ_green / 300 m) ≈ 4.8e-4** — stock exp2 (FX flames/smoke) then equals the physical extinction at Temple distances (0.3 % at 100 m, 1.4 % at 300 m) instead of 16/49/93 % at 50/100/200 m — keep stock fog on (washes out night fires across the Mount).
- **Stock-fog fallback removed from the env fog chunk** — with all-zero uniforms the physical path gives T = 1 (no fog) for a program's first frame instead of heavy stock fog on distant geometry; `mkFogTransmittance()` split out so FX additive emitters can use the env fog (request to render/fx) — keep a fallback.
- **Runtime `quality:change` handled in env** (`applyTier`): sky-view LUT resized (same texture object, so the dome and star shaders keep their references), cube target recreated and the PMREM target dropped (PMREMGenerator never resizes a target it is given), Milky Way/cirrus defines swapped on the existing materials, star geometry rebuilt in place, twinkle toggled; then one synchronous full refresh so no frame samples a disposed target — rebuild env from scratch (re-registers scene objects other tasks may hold).
