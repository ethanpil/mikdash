# Player — decisions

- **Capsule r 0.30 m, h 1.75 m, step-up 0.35 m, eye 1.62 m** — task spec; stairs everywhere are ½ amah rise (24 cm Naeh … 28.8 cm Chazon Ish, report §0 "Units", Middot 2:3), so 0.35 m clears every riser under all three amah standards; the narrowest walkable void in the research, the 3-amah mesibah (1.44 m, Middot 4:5 via report §13.10), still leaves a 0.84 m band for the capsule axis. — Alternative: r 0.25 m for tighter wickets/ta'im doors (sizes unsourced).
- **Collision = one merged world-space triangle soup → one `MeshBVH` (CENTER split, maxDepth 64)** — fewest queries and one shapecast per sub-step; builders already merge per material. — Alternative: per-builder BVHs (faster partial rebuilds, more traversal per query).
- **Horizontal-only push-out with the capsule lifted by the step height, plus a 7-ray ground probe (centre + 6 at 0.22 m) projected to the axis** — ½×½-amah treads (24 cm) are narrower than the capsule radius, so the ring rays "pre-climb" the next tread before the lifted capsule can touch the riser two steps ahead; floors/ceilings never push sideways. Contacts that are mostly vertical (low ceiling, ledge > step) refuse the move, unless the body was already stuck there (lets a teleported observer walk out). — Alternative: classic full 3D push-out (jittery on 45° stairs, slides down ramps).
- **Eye eased toward the body on stairs (τ 0.12 s up / 0.09 s down, max lag 0.6 m) and walking slowed on stairs by 1/(1+0.8·slope)** — reads as a real climb (≈0.8 m/s on ½×½ steps) instead of camera hops. — Alternative: full speed with snapping eye.
- **Head-bob: 1.3 cm vertical per step, 0.7 cm lateral per stride (≈3 cm / 1.3 cm when running), no roll** — subtle by request; default from setting `headBob`, off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Left/Right arrows turn (≈92°/s), A/D strafe** — keyboard-only and accessibility users can look around without a mouse; Up/Down walk like W/S. — Alternative: arrows strafe like A/D.
- **Fly = noclip (no gravity, no collision), 6 m/s, Shift 22 m/s, forward follows the view pitch** — matches `?fly=1` semantics in core/params.js; the Mount is ~240 m across. Leaving fly drops to the floor below with gravity.
- **`?pos=` / `{pos}` targets are EYE positions** (as documented in core/params.js); when a floor lies within −0.6/+0.35 m of the implied feet the observer stands on it, otherwise starts hovering in fly mode. `pos` without Z is treated as `at`; a `pos` whose feet or eye fall inside a solid stands on the nearest floor instead of hovering inside masonry. — Alternative: always fly for `pos`.
- **`{at:[X,Y]}` floor choice: prefer floors at the site's walking datums (Mount 0, Ezrat Nashim 6, Ezrat Yisrael 13.5, Azarah 16, Heichal 22; not the crown), nearest the walker's current height (lowest on spawn)** — at:X,Y inside the Heichal must not land on the roof or the aliyah, and over the underground passage must stay in the court. An explicit Z (`{at:[X,Y,Z]}`, `{at, z}`, `?at=X,Y,Z`) picks the floor nearest Z among ALL standable floors (no datum filter), so the tunnel, upper chambers, ta'im storeys and galleries can be targeted. — Alternative: highest floor (roofs) or lowest (tunnels).
- **Observer is never denied a zone; door leaves/curtains are not collided** (colliders tagged door/leaf/curtain/parochet/nocollide or registered in `ctx.movables` are skipped) — ARCHITECTURE §12.
- **Outside every collider the body keeps a "sane ground" at its current height** (no endless fall), re-checked each step so walking back over real ground resumes normal falling. Only a column with no geometry at all qualifies: a walker inside a solid (column winding parity) or below every floor of its column (leaving fly underground or inside a wall, a teleport into masonry) moves to the nearest standable spot (nav grid node within 8 m, else a ring search of column floors). — Alternative: refuse to leave fly mode there (confusing).
- **Nav grid by rasterizing triangles into columns (Recast-style), not by millions of downward raycasts** — one pass over the triangles gives every surface in every column (multi-level for free: tunnels, stairs under ceilings, upper chambers, up to 4 floors/cell), runs in a dependency-free Worker (~9.7 KB) in ~0.3 s for 590 k cells. A floor needs: not inside a closed solid (winding count), nothing within 1.75 m above, and no steep face within 0.30 m that rises more than 0.35 m + 1.2·distance (so ½-amah risers inside the radius don't block, walls/ledges do). — Alternative: downward `bvh.raycast` per cell (several seconds, still blind to walls).
- **Cell 0.5 m; candidate links up to 0.8 m Δh per cell (0.5 m over 24 cm treads can straddle three risers), every link with Δh > 0.4 m verified by three sub-step floor probes (each ≤ 0.35 m)** — distinguishes ½×½ stairs (45°) from a 0.5–0.8 m ledge or the 10-tefach soreg. Probes use a 2 m bucket grid of walkable triangles inside the worker (no three.js/BVH in the worker). — Alternative: the spec's flat |Δh| ≤ 0.4 m rule, which disconnects every ½×½ staircase at 0.5 m cells.
- **Corner rule for diagonal links + exact link bits per node** — no diagonal through wall corners; links/components computed once in the worker, so queries never raycast.
- **Coarse guidance for long routes**: local components of 8×8-cell blocks form a ~10 k-node graph; each query runs Dijkstra from the goal on it and A* uses h = max(octile, coarse − 2.8 m) × 1.1 — walled courts with few gates made plain A* flood the open Mount (135–186 k expansions, 160–260 ms); guided it is 2.7–9 k expansions, 2–10 ms. Slightly inadmissible (paths may be marginally longer; string pulling removes the zig-zag). — Alternative: full HPA* with refined portals (more memory and code).
- **Cost model**: +35 % on cells next to an edge/wall (paths keep off walls and drops), +0.8 per metre climbed; goal snaps to the nearest reachable cell (≤ 8 m) in the start's component, so unreachable targets (wall tops) end beside them.
- **goTo**: carrot 0.55 + 0.3·v m ahead on the smoothed path, view turns toward 1.8 + 0.6·v m ahead (≤ 2.4 rad/s), pitch follows the ground 4 m ahead (stairs) by 75 %, speed eases in/out (accel 0.9·v, decel 0.45·v) and slows for sharp turns; when the way lies > 1.2 rad (69°) off the view it turns in place first (never walks backwards); stuck > 2.2 s → hop 1.2 m along the path, after 3 hops teleport. Any movement/look input cancels; the promise resolves `true` on arrival (`player:arrived` emitted) or `false`.
- **goTo while flying**: a floor target is reached by coming down onto the nearest walkable cell that is connected to the target AND open straight up to the flier's height (over it first, then straight down), then walking the nav path — never through walls or roofs; no such cell within 16 m → fade-teleport. Only a target in the air (`{pos}` with no floor near, or a Vector3 in the air) is a straight free-flight glide (≥ 5 m/s, ≤ 12 s). — Alternative: an arc above the tallest collider (still passes through roofs for interior targets).
- **Targets the walk cannot reach** (wall tops, the top of a block, a closed room): the nav goal snaps to the nearest reachable cell; when the walk ends > 1 m in plan or > 0.5 m in height from the target, the rest is a fade-teleport, then `player:arrived`. — Alternative: resolve `false` (the UI's "Go" would silently stop short).
- **Speeds**: `instant`/`Infinity` teleport, named speeds and positive numbers walk; anything else walks at 1.4 m/s with one console warning (a typo must never teleport). A cancelled or superseded `instant` does not move the observer after its fade; the veil is lifted by whichever teleport holds it last; each fade is bounded (ms + 0.6 s) so a UI fade that never settles cannot hang `goTo`.
- **Nav build starts two frames after `ready`** (5 s safety timeout) so it never delays first paint; `goTo` before it finishes waits for `ctx.nav.ready` (max 20 s, then teleports).
- **`physics.floorAt(x, z)` with no `fromY` returns the standable walking floor** (same as `standFloor` / `ctx.player.floorAtSite`: datum floors preferred, not roof tops); with `fromY` it stays "highest up-facing surface below fromY" (env's horizon profile passes 2000). People's `setSite(X,Y)` therefore lands kohanim inside the Heichal on its floor, not on the roof. — Alternative: keep the raw query and make every caller pass a height (easy to forget).
- **Column queries by plan barycentrics instead of downward raycasts**: `floorAt`, `floorsAt`, `insideAt` and the 7-ray ground probe test the triangles a thin vertical box meets (one BVH traversal) — exact on shared edges, and a solid's underside flush with the floor below no longer hides that floor (the old re-cast from 1 mm below skipped it). — Alternative: `bvh.raycast` all hits (allocates every hit).
- **Per-frame queries traverse with `BVH.prototype.shapecast` and persistent callback objects** (MeshBVH.shapecast spreads a new callbacks object per call; raycasts allocate hit objects); standing still on unchanged ground (`physics.version`) skips every query; goTo reuses one command object. Measured after warm-up: idle ≈ 0.13 KB/frame (was ≈ 5 KB), walking stairs ≈ 2.2 KB/frame (was ≈ 9.8 KB). The remainder is three-mesh-bvh's three typed-array views per traversal (BufferStack.setBuffer) and boxed doubles in three/three-mesh-bvh math. — Alternative: a private traversal of `bvh._roots` (zero garbage, but tied to an internal layout).
- **Winding**: up/down (floor vs underside) is read from triangle winding. `gatherTriangles` swaps two vertices per triangle when a mesh's `matrixWorld` mirrors (negative determinant); a closed collider with negative signed volume is flipped (and reported once); a collider facing mostly down is reported once. The ground probe accepts either winding (its rays start above the feet, outside solids). — Alternative: double-sided floors everywhere (cannot tell a slab's top from its underside).
- **Pointer-lock spike filter**: a running magnitude of recent mouse events (fed by every event); an event over 250 px that is more than 4× the recent motion is clamped to 250 px, over 1000 px dropped; a sustained fast flick passes in full from its second event (slow devices coalesce a whole frame of motion into one event). — Alternative: drop every event over 250 px (lost genuine flicks on 10–15 fps devices).
- **Nav worker input is one transferred copy of the collider soup** (no second structured clone); collision triangle count and BVH build time are logged once at init and recorded in `ctx.buildTimes['player.bvh']`; > 750 k collider triangles warns (decorative geometry left collidable).
