# UI — the map (task area-map): decision log

- **The map is a modal overlay, not a side card** — near full-screen on desktop (margins 2.2 vw / 2.6 vh, so the scene still frames it), a full-screen sheet on phones and short landscape. While it is open the player's input is off (`ctx.player.setInputEnabled(false)`), the HUD is inert and Tab stays inside. — Alternative: the old 344 px card (too small to label sixty places or to pan and zoom).
- **"Go" closes the map** (Go there, This spot, Watch, double-click, Enter) so the walk is seen in the world; reopening the map during a walk shows the route and a Stop pill. The old desktop rule "map stays open while walking" (`keepMap`) is still supported by `app.goTo` but the map no longer uses it. — Alternative: keep the overlay open (the walk would happen behind a full-screen map).
- **Click selects, double-click goes** — a single click/tap selects the place under it (label, event dot or area) and opens its card; a double-click/double-tap goes at the selected speed (to the clicked spot when the place is at least 60 × 60 amot — the Mount, the courts, the valley — else to the place's observer spot). `src/ui/dev/uitest.mjs` checked "a click on the map starts a walk"; that check now double-clicks. — Alternative: click to go (one mis-click sends the visitor across the Mount).
- **Which places are "upper" / "below" is a table, not inferred from floorZ** — the courts are raised platforms (Mount 0 → Ezrat Nashim 6 → Azarah 16 → Heichal 22) and the site model's zone boxes are envelopes (the Chel's box spans the inner precinct; Har HaBayit's spans −40…140), so no floor-height rule separates "the Azarah on its platform" from "the gallery over Ezrat Nashim". `src/ui/map/model.js` lists the stacked zones of site.js (gezuztra, Beit Avtinas, Beit HaNitzotz, Shushan HaBirah, aliyah, Ulam above its ceiling, roofs; the passage under the Birah, Beit HaTevilah and the two Chuldah tunnels below); an unknown future zone falls back to floorZ > 30 (upper) / < −1 (below) inside the Mount. Stacked places get a dashed (above) or dotted blue (below) outline and an UPPER / ROOF / BELOW tag; a click selects the ground place, whose card offers "Also here: ↑ …" chips. Roof and stand-less outlines (they coincide with the building beneath) are drawn only on selection. — Alternative: a level switcher (one more control for a dozen places).
- **Label anchors are computed, not hand-placed** — each zone's main box (largest box at its floor) is rasterised (≤ 30 cells a side); cells where a click would select that zone are kept; a chamfer distance transform picks the most open cell, centred in its free run. This puts "Har HaBayit" in the open south of the Mount and "Azarah" in the open court south of the Heichal without per-view offsets. The city ("Jerusalem") is named 30 amot south of its observer spot outside the south wall. — Alternative: the old per-view `LABELS` table (only three views, broke on any zoom).
- **Declutter: greedy by priority, screen-space font sizes** — order: selected › places with activity now (by importance) › the observer's place › starting soon › tier (3 always named, 2 once its mark is ≥ 9 px, 1 once ≥ 24 px) › area. Each label tries: two lines with Hebrew, a wrapped long name, one line; at its anchor, nudged above/below it, then just outside its mark (gates on wall lines). Long narrow strips (Ezrat Yisrael, Ezrat Kohanim, between the Ulam and the Mizbeach) take a rotated label. Rects avoid each other, the map controls, the card and the event dots. New labels fade in over 200 ms (none with reduced motion). — Alternative: labels drawn into the cached plan (blurry when scaled, cannot avoid live badges).
- **Map labels show the short English name (the part before the em-dash, without "The") and the Hebrew before its em-dash** — e.g. "Lishkat HaEtz" / "לשכת העץ" for "Lishkat HaEtz — the Kohen Gadol's chamber (Parhedrin)". The full English, the full transliteration (when the English is not already it) and the full Hebrew are in the card. This replaces the previous "Hebrew only in the tooltip" rule: the user asked for names in both. — Alternative: full names (unreadable at plan scale).
- **Plan rendering: three cached rasters + labels live** — a whole-extent raster (≤ 1600 px, the Kidron and Har HaMishchah included), a Mount raster (≤ 2.8 px/amah), and the current view +25 % per side re-rendered at the exact scale 140 ms after the view settles (≤ 8 M device px; dropped when the map closes). While panning or zooming, the finest raster that covers the view is drawn and the coarser ones fill the edges, so motion never waits for a re-render. Text is never rasterised. Measured on the HD 4600 (low tier): view re-raster ≈ 1–3 ms, static layer + labels ≈ 1–3 ms, dynamic layer ≈ 0.4 ms. — Alternative: map tiles (more code, no gain at this plan's complexity).
- **Two canvases** — the base (plan + labels) redraws only when the view, the activity set, the selection or a label fade changes; the dynamic layer (pulses, the observer, route, dots) redraws at ≤ 15 fps when idle and every frame while the view moves. Nothing runs while the map is closed (checked by `maptest.mjs`). — Alternative: one canvas redrawn whole at 15 fps.
- **Activity fill is cut to the place's own area** — a place's boxes are filled, then the boxes of the higher-priority places inside it are erased (`destination-out`), drawing in ascending priority; so "the night watch on Har HaBayit" lights the open Mount, not the courts. Fill alpha falls with area (√(4000 / area), clamped 0.25–1) so a large place does not tint the whole plan. The observer's place gets the same treatment in pale ink, with an outline only when it is small. — Alternative: outline only (weak signal for small chambers).
- **"Soon" = an event starting within 30 sim-minutes in a place with nothing active** — a dashed gold ring and an "N MIN" pill on its label; places with activity now get a count badge and, when the place is big enough on screen (≥ 54 px and ≥ 1.6 px/amah), the most important title as an italic callout.
- **Event times in the card are given in seasonal hours first** — "day 2–8¼ h · ≈ 07:42–13:28" (hours since sunrise / sunset, quarter-hour steps, GRA; civil time with "≈"), consistent with the time panel. — Alternative: civil clock only.
- **One remembered speed** — the card's speed control reads and writes the existing `travelSpeed` setting (shared with the event rows); the Go button names it ("Walk there", "Run there"…). — Alternative: a separate map speed.
- **Keyboard** — the map takes focus on open (role application, described); arrows pan (Shift = farther), + / − zoom, Enter goes to the selection, "/" opens the search. In the search box typing filters (English, transliteration, Hebrew, id; apostrophes and niqqud ignored) and selects the first match, ↑/↓ move the selection (the map flies to it), Enter goes, Esc clears the text first and closes the map second (`panel.onEscape`). M still toggles the map because typing never starts outside the search box. — Alternative: type-anywhere search (M, T, I, G would be swallowed or would close the map mid-word).
- **Places list** — a column on desktop ≥ 1025 px (grouped: Around the Mount, Har HaBayit, Ezrat Nashim, the Azarah, its chambers and gates, the Sanctuary), with "Happening now" first; on narrower screens a "Places" button opens it as an overlay that closes on choice. The list order is a walking order, not alphabetical.
- **Route line** — when a walk is in progress the map asks `ctx.nav.findPath` once for the drawn route (≈ 1 query per walk, the player planned its own) and trims it to the observer as they move; without nav it draws a straight dashed line. Requested: `ctx.player` could expose its active path to save the duplicate query.
- **Phone card is a bottom sheet that scrolls as a whole** (sticky Go footer, drag the grabber down to dismiss); in short landscape the card sits at the side. — Alternative: an inner scroll area (left no room for the event list at 390 px height).
- **A label never covers another place's anchor** (8 px clearance), except a tier-3 name at its own anchor — a click near a place's centre must select that place, not a neighbour's spilled-over label (found by the double-click test: "Kiyor", set beside its 8-amah mark, captured clicks on the Mizbeach). Near the map's edge a label slides inward only onto ground its place owns (task area-map-fix).
- **After a click, the "keep it clear of the card" pan waits 360 ms** (cancelled by a double-click) so the map never moves between the two clicks of a double-click.
- **The valley and the Mount of Olives are named at their observer spots** (the lookout by the Parah's pyre; the valley floor under the causeway) — their boxes cover over a million square amot and their centres mean nothing. Two places with the same short name keep their qualifier ("Sha'ar Chuldah (western)").
- **Access lines use the lead of the `access` text** (`accessLead` in format.js): the first sentence, or the first two when the first is a terse heading — zones-content writes "Kohanim. Israelites only for semichah…" and "As the court. The most-holy offerings…". Shared by the card and the HUD's restricted-place notice (which had been showing just "Kohanim."). — Alternative: first sentence only (says almost nothing for a third of the places).


## Review fixes (task area-map-fix)

- **A label slid in from an off-screen anchor must land on its own ground** — `layoutLabels(…, owns)` checks the slid label's middle and ±35 % of its width with `owns(id, X, Y)` (ground places: `model.groundAt(X, Y) === id`; storeys: inside one of its boxes). The city ("Jerusalem") and the places of over a million square amot are never slid. Before, the test was the zone's main bounding box, and the envelope boxes (the city around everything, Har HaBayit, the Chel) put "Jerusalem", "Har HaBayit" and "Chel" along the bottom edge of views inside the Azarah, and "Jerusalem" inside the Mount on every first open.
- **A newly selected place opens its card at the top** — `card.show()` resets the scroll of the sheet (phones, short landscape) and of the middle section (desktop) when the place changes; re-renders of the same place keep it.
- **Double tap survives the card opening under it** — for 380 ms after a tap selects a place, `.mk-mapv.is-tapguard` turns off pointer events on everything over the map (the card, the controls that slide aside), so the second tap reaches the map. Tap timing uses the events' own time stamps, not the handler's clock, so the first tap's work (rendering the card on the HD 4600) cannot push the second out of the 340 ms window. — Alternative: hold the card back for 350 ms after every tap (a visible lag on every selection). In `maptest.mjs` a "double tap" whose two taps Playwright delivered > 330 ms apart (by time stamp; it waits for each tap to be handled) is retried: that is no double tap, and the card rightly takes the second.
- **The travel pill is placed in script** (`placeTravel`): on the presets' row, right of them and centred on the visible map, when it fits between the presets and the card; else on a row of its own under the presets. Its bottom counts in the top inset (`insets()`), so fits and centring keep clear of it. — Alternative: CSS centring (it covered the presets on phones and on 1280-wide desktops with the card open).
- **The roof is labelled** — a storey's label may also stand 22 / 36 / 52 px above or below its anchor while it stays in its own box: the roof's anchor is 2 amot from the Heichal's, so "Roof of the Heichal · ROOF" now stands under the Heichal's name (and "Aliyah · UPPER" over it). "Also here" also lists any storey that covers the whole place (the roof over the Heichal, the Ulam, Kodesh HaKodashim…), not only storeys of about its size. The roof's outline is still drawn only when selected: it coincides with the building beneath. — Alternatives: an offset anchor (the roof's zone box is the T's bounding box; its free corners are over the Azarah), a chip on the ground label (a second hit target inside a label).
- **Rotated strip labels slide along their strip** (0, ±0.6, ±1.2 × half their length + 12 px) when their anchor spot is taken — e.g. "Ezrat Kohanim" by the off-screen "you" chevron on the axis.
- **The "you" marker and the labels keep apart** — the off-screen chevron's rect is in the labels' blocked list (labels are laid out again when it moves > 6 px); the chevron slides along its edge clear of the map controls and the event markers; an observer whose arrow would sit under a control (the zoom column above a phone's card) is shown by the chevron instead. The on-screen arrow is still drawn over labels (map convention: "you" on top).
- **Toasts hide while the map is open** (`#ui-root.mk-open-map .mk-toasts`; they are about the world and show again on close if still running). The HUD's restricted-place notice raised while the map is open is held and shown when it closes, if the observer is still there. The map's own "no floor to stand on" goes to its live region (the card already says so and disables Go).
- **Focus returns to the HUD map button** on close — `hide()` uncovers the HUD before focusing (focus() does nothing on a `visibility: hidden` button).
- **Touch targets** — with a coarse pointer, on phones and in short landscape: presets, speed segments, Places, Watch, Stop and "Also here" chips ≥ 40 px tall (44 px on portrait phones), list rows 44 px, the close buttons 44 × 44; level tags 10.5 px (canvas and DOM).
- **The map is built before it is first opened** — after `ready`, in idle time: the model, then the label anchors a slice at a time (≤ 12 ms when the frame never leaves idle time; rIC timeout 1 s), then the panel DOM and the list, the label text measurements, and the two coarse plan rasters. Measured on the HD 4600 (low): first open 36–42 ms of main-thread time in five of six runs (was 149–667 ms); one run opened before the warm-up had finished took 406 ms. Opening before it is done finishes the work at once. The map panel now opens with a translate only (a scaled near-full-screen layer is rasterised twice). The remaining long frames after an open come from the GPU: the 3D scene keeps rendering at full cost behind the opaque map; requested a render pause or low rate from engine/render while a full-screen modal is open.
