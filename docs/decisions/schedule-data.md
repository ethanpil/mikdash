# schedule-data — decisions (src/data/schedule.js)

Format: **Choice** — why (source / reasoning) — alternative.

## Order and timing

- **The order of the day follows Mishnah Tamid 1–7, with Abaye's seder (Yoma 33a) for the steps Tamid leaves implicit** (five lamps before the tamid's blood, two after it; limbs → minchah → chavitin → libations → musafim → bazichin → afternoon tamid) — Tamid is the primary narrative; the report's §28 clock is its summary — the report's table alone.
- **Pre-dawn chain anchored on alot; the knock at alot − 70** — Tamid 1:2 / Yoma 1:8 give only "cock-crow, or near it, before or after"; Rambam BhB 8:11 "before dawn, close to it". 70 minutes lets the night work (lottery, inspection, terumat hadeshen, clearing, tapuach, two piles, logs) end just before dawn, where Tamid 2:5–3:2 needs it — knock at a fixed night hour (would drift against dawn across the year).
- **Barkai at alot + 5, after the second lottery** — Tamid 3:1–2 orders lottery then watcher; the report's clock puts it at first light (§28 #4) and the slaughter at sunrise (§28 #5), so the hour between them follows the report, not the Mishnah (said in the timing note). Yoma 28b makes "the whole east lit" later than the first grey — Barkai later (moves the gap to between the second lottery and the watcher instead).
- **Gate-opening blasts and the great gate at sunrise − 7 … − 1, slaughter from sunrise − 1** — the report's clock (§28 #5) puts the opening at sunrise; the lamb, vessels and watering fill the interval after Barkai — gates opened right after Barkai (≈ alot + 30), with the Shema in the Gazit near sunrise.
- **Afternoon incense and Menorah lighting at 9.15–9.45 seasonal hours, between the afternoon blood and the limbs** — Yoma 14b–15a: in the evening the incense precedes the lighting, "at the time of lighting"; the limbs are "offered at 9½" (Pesachim 5:1), and the morning pattern puts the incense between blood and limbs (Yoma 3:4; 33a) — the report's §28 #12 "dusk" (would leave two hours between the tamid and its incense). Both timing notes name the difference; §28 #12 needs an erratum.
- **Locking at tzeit − 15 … + 5; watch posted at tzeit; Ish Har HaBayit's rounds from tzeit + 30 until dawn** — "when the time of locking came" (Middot 1:9) has no hour; the watch is "all night" (Rambam BhB 8:2) and he went round "all the night" (BhB 8:10), so his rounds end at alot while the guards stay until the gates open — locking at sunset; rounds ending at alot − 95 (the first draft, which cut the night short).
- **Sanhedrin and private offerings from the end of the morning song (sunrise + 106) to 8.3–8.4 seasonal hours** — "from the morning tamid to the afternoon tamid" (Sanhedrin 88b); "on it complete all the offerings" (Yoma 33a) — chatzot-based hours.
- **Occasional scenes given one fixed slot** (sleeping guard at night hour 7.5; a kohen's night descent at night hour 6; metzora at day hour 3; nazirites, wood-sorting, hides through the day) — the sim is a pure function of time, so a "possible" scene needs a slot; each is marked 'reconstruction' and its text says it is not nightly/daily — omit them.
- **Mixed anchors checked across the year** — see Validation.

## Places (pos / view)

- **First lottery in Beit HaMoked** — Tamid 1:2–3 narrates the call and the lot before the wicket into the Azarah is opened (the Gazit's holy half lies inside the locked Azarah); the report's clock agrees (§28 #2, "Moked → altar") — the report's §27.2 and §62 (after the Yoma 25a braita): all lotteries in the Gazit. The report contradicts itself here and needs an erratum; Yoma 22a–25a do not place the first lottery.
- **The Barkai watcher and the six Friday blasts on the Water Gate roof walk** — the Gemara puts the watcher on a roof with a second man on the ground (Yoma 28b), but does not say which roof; the report speaks of a lookout and a "roof station" (§28, §33.1). This is a modelled, reachable roof (Beit Avtinas, the KG's first mikveh). The Barkai view looks NE (60°) — a verifier asked for ESE (100–130°) so that "as far as Hebron" (Hebron lies south) is in frame, but the geometry does not allow it: the built roof is walkable only on its north strip (x 216–220.8, y 261.2–262.4; east of the roof mikveh is a void down to +30.5), and from there the Azarah's south wall, rising 20 amot just east of the walk, blocks the east (about 83°–112°) while the roof mikveh's walls hide the low south-eastern horizon (screenshots and a collider probe, 2026-10-19) — the Heichal or ta'im roofs (not daily walking places).
- **Beit Avtinas over the Water Gate (south)** — report lock #20 (Parhedrin north) and §9.3. Yoma 19a itself does not know which of the KG's two chambers was north and leans to Parhedrin in the south, i.e. Avtinas north; the events say so — Avtinas north.
- **Seals reckoning at the NE chamber of Beit HaMoked** — Tamid 3:3 lists a chamber of seals among the Moked's chambers; the report and site.js put it in the NE corner (§9.2, §60). Shekalim 5:4 gives no place. Yochanan and Achiyah are shown in their own clothes (lineage not stated) — anywhere else.
- **Entry circuit staged at the south-side soreg breach #3** — Middot 2:2–3; the breach stations are site.js reconstruction, and Shekalim 6:3's "opposite the 13 gates" is stated in the text.
- **Gevini at the head of the fifteen steps; his cry at the knock** — Rav reads keriat hagever as his cry (Yoma 20b), which the Gemara says was at night; where he stood is unrecorded — omit the event.
- **Morning immersions staged in the underground Beit HaTevilah** — the only mikveh Tamid names off Beit HaMoked (Tamid 1:1); Tamid 1:2 gives no place — a court mikveh.
- **The two morning logs laid before the descent to the Gazit** — Abaye orders them after the incense pile and before the inner clearing (Yoma 33a); Yoma 22a gives them to the first lottery's winner; Tamid 2:5 sends the company to the Gazit after lighting — after the second lottery.
- **Three blasts at the opening of the gates staged at Nikanor** — Sukkah 5:5 gives neither gate nor station.
- **Ben Arza and the trumpeters' second station at the duchan** — Tamid 7:3 says only that they "came and stood by Ben Arza" — near the table of the fats.
- **Shabbat court in the Chel below the Gazit's chol door** — Sanhedrin 88b names the Chel, not the spot.
- **Night-watch focal point in front of the Shushan gate; the incident at the azarah-se post** — posts are site.js `mount.watch-posts` (positions reconstruction).
- **Chavitin bakers' pos in Ezrat Yisrael beside their chamber** — site.js has no zone for azarah.chamber.chavitin (inside it zoneAtSite gives ezrat-yisrael / har-habayit).
- **Every view is [X, Y, Z] with Z = the floor stood on** — several spots are multi-level (roof walk over the Water Gate passage, Ulam over the otem, the underground); player/goto.js and sim/schedule.js accept a third element — [X, Y] only (the player would pick a datum floor).

## Day types and layers

- **Explicit `dayTypes` on every event; Shabbat variants as separate events** (inspection by lamplight, outgoing blessing, psalm 92, musaf + Ha'azinu, showbread exchange / bazichin / division, court in the Chel, incoming watch's afternoon lottery, Az Yashir) — the UI lists them as separate clickable items — one event with day-dependent text.
- **No individual offerings, wood-sorting, hide-salting, nazirite or metzora scenes on Shabbat; the Golah wheel runs on Shabbat** — individual offerings and non-service labour do not override Shabbat; drawing with the Golah wheel is expressly permitted (Eruvin 10:14).
- **Beit Avtinas compounding has `dayTypes: ['occasional']`** — the sources give the yearly quantity (368 maneh = 365 + 3, Keritot 6a), not how often it was compounded (Yoma 38a's daily wages decide nothing: it words Beit Garmu's weekly work the same way); 'occasional' never matches ctx.time.dayType, so it stays off the clock but is available to info panels — a daily slot (would present work of unknown frequency as daily).
- **Bazichin burned after the musafim by two of the incoming mishmar; on Shabbat the morning tamid is shown with nine and the afternoon with eleven** — Abaye's order (Yoma 33a; Yoma 34a: a braita puts the bazichin first, a dispute of Tannaim) and the braita giving the bazichin to the incoming watch (Sukkah 56b). Yoma 2:5 counts the two bazichin bearers with a Shabbat tamid (eleven), which the Gemara reads as the morning one (Yoma 26b); the event says the model departs from that count — bearers walking with the morning tamid.
- **Afternoon tamid crew: on weekdays the morning's winners; on Shabbat the incoming mishmar, by a new lottery** (Yoma 26a) — said in the participant notes.
- **Kodashim meal and seals reckoning on weekdays only** — both come from individuals' offerings, which are not brought on Shabbat (the Shabbat showbread has its own event).
- **No women among the participants of `private-offerings`** — a bird offering needs no leaning or slaughter, so its owner has no reason to enter the Ezrat Kohanim (Kelim 1:8; report §59 #7); women appear in `women-court` and `entry-circuit`.
- **Western lamp: late-era (relit per Tamid 6:1)** — the model's era is late Herodian (report §36.1, lock §58); Yoma 39a–b's Shimon HaTzaddik state is a stylization layer — always-lit.
- **"Bread as warm as when set" as an `aggadah`-layer event** — the tradition reports it as a wonder (Yoma 21a–b) — fold into the exchange text.
- **Participant counts the sources do not give are staging numbers, flagged in `note`** — the people/sim tasks need numbers.

## Validation (2026-10-19; re-run after the verifier fixes, 83 events)

- Every time spec resolves; ctx.time.resolve (src/time, headless) and a tiny §6 resolver (alot 04:42, sunrise 05:56, sunset 16:49, tzeit 17:26) agree on all 166 specs to 0.4 min.
- Durations positive and ≤ 15 h on seven dates (equinoxes, solstices, Jan, May, Nov); 67 end-before-start and 5 start-before-start constraints of the sources (e.g. vessels before the watering, five lamps before the blood, the afternoon lottery before 8½ hours, Friday blasts before the locking) and 7 "inside" constraints hold on all seven dates.
- No crew (memuneh, first winner, altar company, slaughterer, thrower, clearing men, the nine, incense and pan men, trumpeters, segan, Kohen Gadol, choir, ma'amad, Sanhedrin, outgoing mishmar, afternoon crew…) is in two zones at once outside one choreography.
- Every zone id exists; every pos lies in its event's zone (zoneAtSite); every view lies on its zone's floor and outside site.js solids — and, in the running app, on a standable collider floor at its Z, not inside masonry, on the nav grid and reachable from the `start` anchor (80/80).
- Spot screenshots (low tier) of the Gazit, Barkai roof, slaughter ring, Ulam steps, Moked and the sleeping-guard post frame their scenes; no console errors with the schedule loaded by the sim.
