# UI — decision log

- **Time, info and map are non-modal side drawers; only Settings is modal** — scrubbing the clock should show the sky, light and people change live, so the scene must stay visible. On phones every panel becomes a bottom sheet (≤ 76 dvh) that leaves the upper view visible. — Alternative: a centred modal for everything (simpler, but hides the effect of the time change).
- **Backdrop blur only on `high`/`ultra`** — `backdrop-filter` over a WebGL canvas that redraws every frame re-blurs every frame; on the HD 4600 class (`low`/`medium`) the glass is a near-opaque warm gradient (97–98 %, so neither the 3D view nor HUD text reads through it) instead. — Alternative: blur everywhere (prettier, costs several ms per frame on iGPUs).
- **The seasonal dial is uniform in sha'ot zmaniyot** — 24 equal sectors: the 12 day hours across the top (sunrise on the left, midday at the top, sunset on the right), the 12 night hours across the bottom. The Mishnah counts in these hours (Pesachim 5:1 "eight and a half hours"), so its moments sit at fixed angles all year while the civil times move. GRA reckoning (sunrise→sunset), as `ctx.time` computes it. Dawn (−16.1°) and nightfall (−8.5°) are marked as diamonds; the sky colour of the ring follows the day's zmanim. — Alternative: a 24 h civil clock face with zmanim marked on it (familiar, but hides the very system the user wants to explore).
- **Civil times always carry "≈", and every event shows its timing confidence** (sourced / order sourced, minutes reconstructed / reconstructed) — the sources fix the order of the service and a few seasonal hours, not clock minutes (ARCHITECTURE §9). — Alternative: show bare HH:MM (reads as if sourced).
- **Sanctity ladder numbering is detected, not assumed** — the report's §0.5 counts Eretz Yisrael as 1 (Har HaBayit 4, Heichal 10, Kodesh HaKodashim "above all"); the Rambam (BhB 7:13–22) counts walled cities as 1 (Har HaBayit 3, KhK 10). The UI draws 11 named rungs (0 = Eretz Yisrael … 10 = KhK) and reads the data's numbering from Har HaBayit's value (3 → Rambam, otherwise the report); the zone id `kodesh-hakodashim` always maps to the top rung. The HUD names the rung ("Sanctity of Ezrat Nashim") instead of printing a number. Fixtures use the report's numbering. — Alternative: hard-code one numbering (breaks silently if zones-content picks the other).
- **Fixtures only while the real data is missing, and labelled "Sample"** — `src/ui/fixtures.js` (13 events, 16 zones, names for all canonical zones) is used only when `ctx.schedule.events` is empty / `ctx.zones` has no content; the panels print a "Sample" note then. Every fixture line was checked against Tamid, Middot and Rambam BhB 7 in `../sources`; e.g. the second lottery and Shema are placed in Lishkat HaGazit per Tamid 2:5 and 4:3. Clock minutes in fixtures are reconstruction. — Alternative: lorem-ipsum fixtures (faster, but the design would not meet real text lengths).
- **No side of the Azarah is stated for Lishkat HaGazit in UI text** — printed Middot 5:3–4 vs the Mahari/Chanukat HaBayit reading (report §53) disagree; the site model's placement is a lock, the prose stays neutral.
- **Sefaria links only where the mapping is unambiguous** — "Tractate c:m" → `Mishnah_<T>.c.m`, "Tractate 21b" → Bavli `<T>.21b`, "Rambam BhB c:h" → `Mishneh_Torah,_The_Chosen_Temple.c.h`, Torah/Tanakh by English or Hebrew book name; ranges link to their first reference; numbered books link in either form ("1 Kings 6:2", "II Chronicles 3:1"), and a Rambam book may be written "Hilchot X" (a leading "Hilchot" also marks the citation as Rambam); everything else (EK, SG, Tosefta, Yerushalmi, Rambam books without a verified Sefaria title such as Hilchot Shekalim) is plain text. Every title spelling (Arakhin, Berakhot, Pirkei_Avot, Vessels_of_the_Sanctuary…, Daily_Offerings…, Admission_into_the_Sanctuary) was verified against the Sefaria API on 2026-10-19. — Alternative: link everything through Sefaria search (always resolves, but lands on a results page).
- **The Hebrew date follows the halachic day** — `ctx.time.hebrewAt(t)` (the day begins at sunset); after sunset a badge says the Hebrew date has turned. The psalm of the day (Tamid 7:4) is shown for the civil weekday.
- **Compass shows a gold mark toward Kodesh HaKodashim** — an orientation aid computed from the player's site position to the KhK centre read from `ctx.site.zones['kodesh-hakodashim']` (not a constant); the bearing is recomputed when the heading or the position (≈ 0.5 m steps) changes, so it stays true while walking straight. — Alternative: cardinal points only.
- **Floating tags over active events (setting "Floating labels")** — at most 4, projected each frame with no DOM writes unless the value changed; clicking one opens the hour panel at that event. A tag below 20 % opacity leaves the tab order (`tabindex -1`, `aria-hidden`); tags come last in the HUD's tab order and paint under the other HUD pieces (`z-index: -1`); they are hidden (and not projected) while any panel is open. — Alternative: no in-world labels (the setting would have nothing to switch).
- **Per-row travel speed is sticky** — choosing a speed on any event row also becomes the default (`travelSpeed`) used by map clicks. — Alternative: per-row only (users re-pick it every time).
- **Quality / amah changes reload in place** — the page reloads with `pos`, `look`, `t`, `date` (and without `q`/`amah`, which would override the saved value) and skips the intro via a sessionStorage flag. — Alternative: reload to the default dawn spawn.
- **Screenshot mode ignores stored settings** — `shot=1` renders must be deterministic; core still reads `mikdash.quality`/`mikdash.amah` itself.
- **`ctx.ui.fade` has two forms** — `fade(to 0..1, ms)` matches what `src/player/goto.js` already calls for teleports; `fade(ms)` dips to black, resolves when black and lifts by itself (the ARCHITECTURE task wording).
- **Audio is unlocked only from a trusted gesture** (Enter key or click on the intro); API paths leave it to audio/'s own first-gesture unlock, so no autoplay warnings.
- **UI code loads only when the UI is on** — `src/ui/index.js` creates `ctx.settings` (other systems read it in every mode) and dynamically imports `app.js` + `styles.css` only when `params.ui`.
- **Web fonts are a script-inserted `<link>`, never an `@import` in the UI stylesheet** — `styles.css` is the CSS of a lazy chunk, and Vite's preload helper resolves the `import('./app.js')` only after that CSS `<link>` fires `load`, which waits for every `@import`; a slow or blackholed fonts.googleapis.com therefore stalled `init` and the whole boot (verified on a production build + preview with the fonts host held open: stuck at "Preparing ui…" before, boots in ~7 s after). `index.js` appends preconnects + the stylesheet without awaiting them (`display=swap`, Georgia/David until the faces arrive); the `__mikdashReady` wait on `document.fonts.ready` is capped at 5 s. — Alternative: `<link media=print onload>` in index.html (orchestrator-owned; same effect).
- **The UI sets `window.__mikdashReady` in non-shot mode** (30 frames after `ready`, after fonts) so `tools/shot.mjs --keep-ui` does not time out; main.js sets it only for `shot=1`. The orchestrator may move this into main.js.
- **Names in the HUD drop the em-dash subtitle on phones** ("Har HaBayit — the Temple Mount" → "Har HaBayit") so the location card and the time chip fit side by side at 390 px.
- **Map labels in English, Hebrew in the hover tooltip** — at plan scale a second script doubles the clutter; the Hebrew stays one hover away and in the HUD. *(Superseded: the map now labels both, decluttered by zoom — see ui-map.md.)*
- **Minimap source: `ctx.site` only** — element boxes by kind and elevation (walls, gates in gold, chambers, floors tinted by datum), the soreg drawn from `params.path`, stoa columns from `params.columnRowsFromWall/bay/gateGaps`, stair treads from `params.ascentHeading/steps`, the fifteen steps from `params.stepRadii`, the 24 rings from `params.positions`. Aloft and underground zones are skipped in the 2D hover lookup. Rendered once per view/size; only the arrow and markers redraw (≤ 15 fps, only while open). *(The plan renderer moved to src/ui/map/plan.js with continuous pan/zoom — see ui-map.md.)*
- **HUD pieces under an open panel are measured, not listed per layout** — on open/resize, `app.js` intersects the panel's layout box (`offset*`: its open transform is `none` in every layout, so this is its final place even mid-slide; the centred modal is measured as drawn) with the location card, time chip, tool group and compass, and fades the overlapped ones out (`visibility: hidden`, so they also leave the tab order). One rule covers desktop drawers, the map card, phone sheets and short landscape. — Alternative: a media-query matrix per side and size (brittle as panels change).
- **Desktop time drawer docks the tool buttons beside it** — the chip it replaces is hidden, the Info/Map/Settings column slides to the drawer's left edge (still clickable), the compass steps aside, and captions/status re-centre in the free part of the view for every drawer/card. — Alternative: start the drawer below the chip (costs ~60 px of dial and list height).
- **Time panel order: dial → transport → one-line date → Happening now → Next → zmanim + explanation** — the key flow (what is happening, Go) must be above the fold at 1280×720 and 390×844. The quarter-hour count moved into the dial readout ("10¾ hours since sunset"), the hour lengths into the Seasonal hours teaser, the GRA explanation into the `<details>`. The dial shrinks with the viewport height (`min(290px, 100vh − 460px)`, 220 px on phones). The native date input sits under the compact date button and opens via `showPicker()`. — Alternative: tabs inside the panel (one more click for the main use).
- **Event arcs are lane-packed** — greedy packing (sorted by start, first of three concentric tracks with no circular overlap incl. a 0.06 h gap); events longer than 2 seasonal hours (the night watch, the Sanhedrin) are a thin, faint underlay on the innermost track; arcs are trimmed 0.04 h at both ends with butt caps so neighbours never merge. Pointing at an arc (or focusing it, or a first tap on touch) shows its title and time in the dial centre; a click / second tap / Enter sets the clock. The arcs are one roving tab stop (arrows move between them), separate from the ring slider. — Alternative: a single track with tooltips (unreadable once the real schedule fills it; tooltips do not exist on touch).
- **Crossing into a restricted place is announced** (ARCHITECTURE §12: the observer may walk anywhere, "the UI informs") — entering rung ≥ 7 (Ezrat Kohanim and inward) from a lower rung shows a toast with the place's Hebrew name, the first sentence of its `access` text (else the ladder rule) and the Mishnah (Kelim 1:8 for Ezrat Kohanim, 1:9 for between the Ulam and the Mizbeach, the Heichal and Kodesh HaKodashim, per report §0.5); the Heichal and KhK add "you see it here as an observer". At most once per zone per 90 s (boundary flapping), not during the intro, off when subtitles are off; the location card's border turns red-gold there. — Alternative: only the rung glyph (hidden on phones; misses the research's central teaching).
- **Phone sheets can be dragged down to dismiss** (grabber or header, past 25 % of the sheet height; a shorter drag springs back) — the grabber promised the gesture. — Alternative: remove the grabber.
- **Toasts sit above panels** (`z-index`), below the HUD on phones (clear of the location card and the tool column) and between the HUD pieces in short landscape. — Alternative: bottom toasts (hidden by phone sheets).
- **Event times are cached by event id** — `ctx.schedule.active()/upcoming()` may return fresh objects each call; precomputed numeric `t0/t1` bypass the cache entirely. — Alternative: cache by object (grows without bound at 14 Hz).
