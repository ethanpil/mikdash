# Decisions — site-model-fix (verification pass on `src/data/site.js`)

Format: **choice** — why (source / reasoning) — alternative. ⚙ = reconstruction-class. The disposition of every reviewer
finding is in `docs/site-model.md` §9; this file records the non-obvious choices made while applying them.

## Labels
- **An element carries the confidence of the weakest number that defines its box; `params.fieldConfidence` keeps the sourced parts.** — ARCHITECTURE §0: anything the sources do not fix is `reconstruction`. A builder reading `explicit` on an EN chamber whose height is ours would be misled. `runChecks()` now fails any element that carries ⚙ without `reconstruction` or a fieldConfidence entry naming the ⚙ part. — Alternative: per-field labels only, keeping the old element labels (hides the ⚙ from anyone reading `confidence`).

## Har HaBayit
- **Shushan HaBirah chamber modeled over the gate, +22 → +28, within the wall's thickness; the two standard amot hang inside it at its NE and SE corners** ⚙. — Kelim 17:9 puts the amot "in Shushan HaBirah, on the NE and SE corners"; the report draws "the chamber of the two standard amot" above the gate (Part X §48, EK I 167–168; Part XIV §65.4). Its top is kept at the EN east wall's +28, so under real topography the Parah line loses nothing (the chamber could rise to ≈+43 at 48 cm). — Alternative: no chamber (the earlier choice), which keeps the Gemara's level-line band +25–26 (Yoma 16a; Rambam BhB 6:5 "the wall over this gate low") but drops a placement the report states twice. The level-line model still sees 11.99 amot with the chamber.
- **Low east walls cited as "low (Middot 2:4; Rambam BhB 6:5); +gate + 2 chosen ⚙"**, labelled reconstruction. — EK I 174's "not less than 22" is his minimum for the *high* walls and names the eastern wall as the exception, so it cannot fix the low wall's height. — Alternative: none needed; only the citation changed.
- **N and S Mount-wall feet follow a profile that drops to the lower E/W grade at each corner over 40 m** ⚙. — The corner columns belong to the N/S walls, whose own grades (−4 and −27 at 48 cm) are far above the Kidron (−58) and Tyropoeon (−37.5) grades, which left open corner columns. — Alternative: hand the corners to the E/W walls (fixes the hole but leaves a 15–30-amah cliff in the foot right at the corner).
- **Lishkat HaKorban and Lishkat HaParochet stand on the Mount just outside the soreg, flanking the foot of the Korban gate's flight; 14 × 12 × 12** ⚙. — They are Levite watch posts (Middot 1:1; Rambam BhB 8:9) and Levites guard outside the Azarah (BhB 8:4, 8:8); the curtains were woven by women (Ketubot 106a), who could not enter the courts; Lishkat HaKorban sits by the gate the kodshei kodashim animals used (Middot 1:5). — Alternative: inside the Azarah along the N wall (then a Levite post inside the court, and no women's access to the curtain shop).
- **Lishkat Beit HaEven on the Mount NE of the court complex (X 365–385, Y 432–447), door facing north** ⚙. — Parah 3:1: "the chamber facing the Birah, to the north-east"; the Birah's site is open (§20 #8), but the model's underground passage runs north toward it. — Alternative: NE corner of the Mount by the north stoa.
- **24 night-watch posts placed at the named places** (the 5 Mount gates inside their openings, the Mount's corners inside, the 5 Azarah gates on the top tread outside, the Azarah's corners outside on the chel, the two chamber doors, the 11-amah strip at the lul) ⚙. — Middot 1:1; Rambam BhB 8:4–9 ("on the five gates of the Azarah, outside the Azarah, since the kohanim guard the Moked and Nitzotz gates"). The kohanic Moked post is in the hall's chol half. — Alternative: SG's 10-per-post inflation (Part X §53), not used.
- **Soreg breach stations: even spread kept, then moved ≥ 2 amot clear of any passage**. — Shekalim 6:3 says the prostrations were "opposite the thirteen gates" and EK draws the breaches there, but on a soreg 10 amot out every such spot is a flight's passage. — Alternative: put the stations at the passages' edges (a reading of "opposite"); not adopted, as it still reads as a gap.

## Ezrat Nashim
- **EN corner-chamber walls 22 high (top +28)** ⚙. — The unroofed NE and SE chambers sit against the low east wall (+28); 25 made their partitions stand above it. — Alternative: a 3-amah step above the outer wall, recorded as deliberate.
- **Lishkat Chasha'im in Ezrat Nashim, built into its N wall under the north gallery (X 320–335, Y 394–403, Z 6–16; door 4 × 6)** ⚙. — Shekalim 5:6: the God-fearing give secretly and the poor of good families are supported secretly; EN is open to men and women alike. The gallery floor (+16) is its roof. — Alternative: beside Lishkat HaKelim in the Azarah (closed to women and to most of the poor).

## Azarah
- **Ezrat Kohanim paved by `azarah.floor` running to X 242 (the duchan's foot)**. — Middot 5:1 / BhB 5:12 count the 11 amot of the mekom drisat haKohanim as court; the zone element stays as the EK zone. — Alternative: a separate EK floor slab (same geometry, one more id).
- **North trio re-laid at the NW: Etz (Parhedrin) 30 × 7 inside the court + the 5-amah wall, X 68–98, Y 391–403; Gazit 45 × 20 straddling the W wall at Y 371–391, in front of it; Golah 10 × 10 at X 88–98, Y 381–391, also in front of it** ⚙. — Middot 5:3 "six chambers in the Azarah"; Middot 5:4 / Rambam BhB 5:17 "the Etz behind both, the roof of the three level"; Yoma 11a "Sha'ar Nikanor, inside of which was Lishkat Parhedrin" (it is in the court); Yoma 10a (a dwelling with a mezuzah) needs it chol, which Rambam BhB 6:7 gives a chamber built in kodesh that opens only to chol. EK reaches the same "inside the Azarah, opening to chol, behind Golah and Gazit" (Part X §52). The Gazit is 20 deep (was 25) to keep a 5½-amah passage from the Heichal's north wall for the west inspection company (Tamid 1:3); its kodesh door (Y 371–381) faces open court south of the Golah. — Alternatives: Etz straddling the N wall (half built in chol, still floats); Raavad's Parhedrin "east" near Nikanor (Part X §52, and Rashi's reading of Yoma 11a), recorded.
- **Gazit's W-wall straddle kept and flagged as a reading of lock #7.** — Yoma 25a (Abaye): lottery in its east in kodesh, elder in its west in chol ⇒ a N–S dividing line with kodesh to the east; on the Azarah's outline only the W wall has that, and at its north end the chamber is in the north of the court. — Alternative: straddling the N wall (lock #7 read literally), which makes the halves N/S and puts the lottery in the south; recorded in `params.variants.nWallStraddle`.
- **Substructures (podiums) under the Gazit's chol half and the north part of Beit HaMoked, solid from Z 0 to +16** ⚙. — Both floors are at +16 outside the court walls, over a Mount at 0. — Alternative: vaulted kippin (EK's mechilot) instead of solid masonry; same envelope.
- **Lishkat HaKelim at the NE corner of the court, 7 into Ezrat Yisrael + the N wall (X 244–255, Y 391–403), floor +13½** ⚙. — Lock §58 asks for a nominal box. Tamid 3:3–4: the dawn kohanim took the 93 vessels from it right after the lamb, so it is near the Moked; Shekalim 5:6: any donor throws vessels in, and Israelite men reach EY. — Alternative: on the court perimeter near the Moked inside the Tzafon (eats the slaughter ground).
- **The S wall stops at the Water Gate house roof (+36) over its 30-amah span**, so Beit Avtinas and the KG's first mikveh stand on an open roof terrace; a spiral stair in the gatehouse's east room gives access ⚙. — Tamid 1:1 / Rambam BhB 8:5: Beit Avtinas is an upper chamber "on" the gate; Yoma 31a: the mikveh is "on the roof of the Water Gate". The 12½-amah court margin (BhB 5:15) keeps the house inside the wall line. — Alternative: the wall continuing to +56 with the rooms as voids inside it (no roof, no access).
- **Rooms built into walls listed as `voids`; the hall/basilica that replace a wall as `replacedBy`; explicit boxes as `cutouts`.** — Builders cut only what a wall lists; `runChecks()` now fails any chamber, gate, opening or fixture inside a court wall that is not listed. — Alternative: one `openings` list for everything (loses the difference between a room inside the wall and a gap through it).
- **Beit HaMoked corner chambers 5 (along the S/N walls) × 6** ⚙, so the 10-amah gate and the chel door each open onto a clear 10-amah passage. — Middot 1:6 "like small bedrooms"; the 20-amah interior cannot hold four 6-wide corner rooms and two 10-wide doors. — Alternatives: widen the hall (lock §58 fixes 24 × 24) or thin its walls to 1.
- **R. Yehudah's pishpash is a wicket in the gate's east leaf (2 × 4)** ⚙. — Middot 1:7 "the door open to the Azarah had a small pishpash"; Tamid 1:3 and Rambam BhB 8:11 ("he opened the small gate between the Beit HaMoked and the Azarah") need it to open from the hall into the court; beside the gate it could only open into a corner chamber. — Alternative: beside the gate with corner chambers ≤ 3½ wide.
- **Keys slab moved to the hall's chol half (Y 403–404).** — The officer "put his garment on it and slept on it" (Middot 1:9), and there is no sitting, let alone sleeping, in the kodesh (Yoma 25a). — Alternative: kodesh half (the earlier spot; contradicts the sleeping).
- **Moked kodesh/chol line at Y 402 justified only by lock #43 (half / half).** — The wall-thickness rule (Rambam BhB 6:9) belongs to a standing wall; here the hall replaces it and the rashei pispasin mark the line (Middot 1:6). — Alternative: Y 403 (13 | 11) by the wall-thickness rule.
- **The amah: an open channel 1 × 1 from the altar's SW corner west of the yesod ramp, south to 7 amot below the kevesh foot, east to the Water Gate, then covered under its threshold and under the Mount to the east wall** ⚙. — Middot 3:2 (the yesod bloods mix in the amah and go out to the Kidron); Tamid 5:5 (the spilled coals at the kevesh are swept into it); Pesachim 5:8 (the court flushed); REbY on the Water Gate "through it the water trickles" (Middot 2:6). — Alternative: an underground drain only (loses the open channel the coals are swept into).
- **Kalpi station in Ezrat Kohanim at (238.5, 345)** ⚙. — Yoma 3:9 [37a]: "to the east of the Azarah, to the north of the altar … and the kalpi was there". — Alternative: in Ezrat Yisrael (east of the duchan); EK keeps it beside the altar.
- **The dawn inspection circuit is a route (two polylines), not a built colonnade** ⚙. — Tamid 1:3 names an achsadra and Rambam BhB 1:9 allows stone exedras, but no source places or sizes one; a perimeter colonnade would cross the gatehouses, the chambers and the explicit 8-amah strip by the nanasin. — Alternative: a 5-amah colonnade along the court walls (unsourced; collides).
- **Shofar-chests not placed.** — Shekalim 6:1, 6:5 say "in the Mikdash" only; the familiar "treasury in the Court of the Women" is from Josephus and the New Testament, which ARCHITECTURE §0 excludes. — Alternative: 13 stations along the EN walls, if the orchestrator wants props there as reconstruction.

## Mizbeach
- **Yesod ramp's level run extended to X 201**, so it meets the S stub and the W yesod along 2 amot. — Zevachim 62b; Rambam BhB 2:14 (a small ramp to the yesod). — Alternative: turning north onto the 1-amah stub only.
- **Kevesh given as a 30-amah ground piece + a 2-amah flying span (undersides +17 over the yesod line, +22 over the sovev)**. — Rambam BhB 2:13: 30 on the ground, flying 1 over the yesod and 1 over the sovev. — Alternative: a single box (it extrudes into the altar body).
- **Flaying stations in the 7 gaps between the nanasin; the 8 marble tables stay in Rambam's band.** — Rambam BhB 5:13 "there they hang and flay"; Middot 3:5 "on marble tables between the pillars"; Middot 5:2 "rings → tables 4, tables → nanasin 4" puts the tables south of the pillars. — Alternative: the tables among the pillars (Middot 3:5 literal), recorded as a variant.
- **Ulam stair kept at 3, 3, 3, 4 (run 21) with a 1-amah apron at the foot** ⚙ — flagged. Twelve ½ rises in Middot 3:6's "amah, amah, rovad" groups need four groups; Yoma 4:3's "fourth rovad". Lock #25 lists "3/3/4" (the sizes in the Mishnah's order) and the report's "run 22" only follows from R. Yehudah's 5. — Alternatives: R. Yehudah's top 5 (run 22, no apron); a wider top landing instead of the apron.

## Heichal
- **Ta'im stories of 11 → roofs at +55** ⚙, and a 24-step flight along the south roof's outer edge, then a landing slab (open below) to the aliyah door at +67 ⚙. — Part VII §22 F1: the windows are "high in the Kodesh walls (above the ta'im roofline)"; with roofs at +67 they opened into the ta'im. Middot 4:5 describes the route (up the mesibah to the roofs, along the W and S, to the door) but never says it is level. The flight keeps 8½ amot off the window wall and the landing passes above the windows (+66), so the light still radiates out. — Alternatives: roofs at +67 with F1 moved into the aliyah block (but then F1 lights the aliyah, not the Kodesh); 5-amah stories after 1 Melachim 6:10 (Shlomo's yatzia), not used for a 40-amah Kodesh.
- **Ta'im roof pieces leave the mesibah (roofless, EK II 180–182) and the water-descent house (a roof-rain chase, lock #41) open, with a 4-amah landing only at the mesibah's NW head.** — Alternative: roofing the voids (closes the rain chase).
- **Beit hachalifot wickets are sanctity openings (`walkThrough: false`)**, no stair. — Zevachim 55b: they validate the court flanks; their sill (+22) is 6 above the court and no stair is attested. — Alternative: a 12-step flight outside each (unsourced).
- **Aliyah door X 132–142 re-derived: centred on the aliyah's part over the Kodesh (X 117–157), not over the KhK.** — One enters the upper storey over the Kodesh; the rashei pispasin mark the KhK line above (Middot 4:5).
