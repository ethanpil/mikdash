# build-altar — decisions

Scope: `src/world/altar/**`: the Mizbeach, the kevesh and its satellites, the altar top and the three ma'arachot, the
slaughter zone, the kiyor and muchni, the twelve Ulam steps, running water in the amah. Every position and dimension
comes from `src/data/site.js` (`altar.*`, `azarah.amah-channel`). Each bullet below is a choice the sources leave open.
Format: **choice**: why (source / reasoning); the alternative. Revised in build-altar-fix after the accuracy and
visual reviews (marked *fix*).

## Method
- **Each tier of the altar is one swept skin: a profile of (offset, height) points carried round the plan ring (rounded rectangle, or the yesod's L-polygon with per-edge offset weights).** The altar was cast in timber frames and limed, not built of dressed courses (Zevachim 54a–b; Rambam BhB 2:16), so it reads as continuous plastered masses with softened arrises. Fillets are 0.1 amah on top edges, plan corners have a 0.14 radius, and there is a cove where the sovev meets the upper body. Alternative: sharp boxes. Detail 0 keeps sharp edges.
- **The chut hasikra is a band of `sikra_red` inside the same skin (Z 20.95–21.05 from site.js), not an overlay.** This avoids depth-fighting at distance (camera near 0.08 m). Alternative: a painted decal strip.
- **Detail ≥ 2: the wall faces of the altar and the kevesh are tessellated and pushed in and out by up to 1.2 cm (low-frequency noise), horizontally only.** Cast, hand-limed work is not machine-true, and the wavering silhouette is what sells scale. Tops, caps and walking surfaces stay flat and sealed. *fix:* along the S side (X from the yesod's W face − 1 to the sovev ramp + 1, within 1.5 amot of the 30-square's S face, faded over an amah) the faces may only waver **inward**, so no vertex closes the kevesh's hair's-breadth gap; a node check confirms no altar-skin vertex lies inside the flying-span or small-ramp end boxes grown by half a hair. The kevesh's own displacement depends on Y only and fades to zero where the small ramps abut. Alternative: clamp to |d| < half a hair (less waver), or perfectly planar faces.
- **Walking surfaces (the kevesh and the small ramps, the level yesod branch, the sovev, the horn ring and walkway) take `lime_whitewash_worn` once the materials task adds it (requested: foot-polished, no field-stone relief); meanwhile the altar's own `lime_whitewash`.** *fix:* the earlier `plaster` fallback's trowel crazing read as a crazy-paved ramp, contradicting a kevesh cast and limed with the altar (Middot 3:3–4). The plain whitewash still shows its field-stone relief at grazing moonlight; the worn key will cure that. The revuvah cavity is the same lime (the `plaster` draw call is gone). Alternative: plaster.
- **Signs of use, restrained (*fix*): `lime_whitewash_soot` on the top 0.6 amah of the upper body and the horns' sides; `lime_whitewash_stained` on the yesod's top and its S and W faces (the shirayim — Zevachim 53a), on the corner faces within 0.9 amah of the NE and SW corners for 3 amot below the line (the lower bloods — Tamid 4:1), and on the horns' heads (the chatat "on the horns").** The altar was limed twice a year and wiped every Erev Shabbat "because of the blood" (Middot 3:4; Zevachim 54a), so a working altar carries faint soot and runs between washings. Both keys are requested from materials and fall back to the plain whitewash (no extra draw calls until they exist, +2 after). Alternative: a pristine altar.
- **UVs (*fix*):** mounds and drifts (tapuach, ash floor, coal beds, Beit HaDeshen) use plan UVs on every vertex, because the per-vertex projection of `applyWorldUV` smeared a pale seam across smooth indexed surfaces (slopes ≤ 40°, so the plan stretch is ≤ 1.3×). Lathes (kiyor, cups, vessels, nanas shafts, table legs) keep their own UVs in metres (arc length round × along the profile), with the one seam at φ = 0 (south — toward the muchni for the kiyor). The wheel rim keeps the torus's UVs in metres so the grain runs round it. Alternative: non-indexed faces with per-face projection.
- **One StaticBatcher for the whole area (one draw call per material); separate meshes only for movables, the shaft mouth and the animated water.** 27 meshes at every tier (25 drawn while the kiyor stands raised: the bail and the shaft mouth are hidden). Alternative: instanced meshes per part family.

## The Mizbeach
- **Yesod built from `altar.yesod params.pieces` as one L-shaped skin with the two 1-amah stubs; the edges buried against the body are not skinned.** Middot 3:1, as site.js reads it. The SE has no yesod. Alternative: four boxes.
- **Karkov (lock #19, both), *fix — reading corrected*:** Zevachim 62a: the braita defines the karkov as "between horn and horn, the place of the kohanim's feet, an amah"; the gemara objects from "under its karkov below, to its half", which needs a karkov at mid-height; RNbY answers "there were two: one for beauty, and one for the kohanim, that they not slip". So **(1) the kohanim's karkov is the 1-amah walkway on top itself** (altar.walkway, flush, foot-worn; nothing added to it), and **(2) the ornamental karkov ("lenoi", Rebbi's kiyur) is the one of "to its half": a modest cast band on the 30-square just above the chut hasikra (the half) and under the sovev — cavetto foot, flat fascia, half-round roll, weathered top, Z 21.15–21.77, projecting 0.13 amah.** R' Yosi b. R' Yehudah reads the karkov as the sovev, which this placement sits directly under; Shekalim 8:8 puts limbs "beneath the karkov, below". The upper body (+22 to +25) stays plain. The band is a moulding, so detail ≥ 1 only (at 0 its thin underside aliased into a dashed line). It is **broken across the kevesh and the sovev ramp** (X kevesh − 2 hairs … ramp + 2 hairs) and returns into the face with flat ends, so the hair's breadth of air before the altar stays open (Zevachim 62b). The previous kerb on the walkway (1 tefach × 2 etzba'ot) was an invention and is gone. Alternative: a rounded nosing on the sovev's outer edge (it would have to break at the sovev ramp's landing too).
- **The horn ring and the walkway are flush at +25 (site.js), lime-washed; the 24 × 24 ma'arachah place is an ash floor.** Middot 3:1 gives the widths only. Alternative: a sunken hearth.
- **Keranot: hollow (Zevachim 54b, "the stones of the keranot were hollow"), open at the top, walls about 1 tefach, hollow 0.62 deep; corner radius matched to the altar's so they sit flush on the 28-square.** Alternative: solid cubes.
- **Shitin: two round holes of 3 etzba'ot (radius 0.06 amah), 0.28 apart, on the SW yesod top, with dark throats.** "Like two thin nostrils" (Middot 3:2). The size is a choice. Alternative: slots.
- **Libation cups: two silver bowls on low feet in the site box east of the SW horn; the western one (water) has a narrow spout hole and the eastern one (wine) a wide one.** Sukkah 4:9 and 48b; lock #29. Bowl form ⚙. Alternative: plaster bowls (R' Yehudah).
- **The shit slab: marble, 1 amah, standing 1 cm proud of the pavement, with an iron ring and staple.** Middot 3:3. It matches build-courts' keys slab (Part IX §37.1: three identical fittings). Alternative: flush.

## The altar top and the fires
- **Tapuach (*fix*): heaped ash at its angle of repose — a broad, slightly flattened crown (radius ≈ 1.2), straight flanks of tan 0.84 (40°), and a soft talus toe running 0.6 amah past site.js's radius into the floor drift; irregular low-frequency lobes, lumps (d ≥ 1), slumps and six shovel scoops (d ≥ 2), all sampled in plan so the crown has no pinched star; at least 8 rings at detail 0. Small charcoal chunks are scattered over its flanks and toe (d ≥ 1).** Tamid 2:2 (an "apple" mound, "at times ~300 kor"). site.js's 3 high on a radius of 4 leaves no room for the 32–35° a loose heap would take; the toe absorbs the rest. Alternative: a dome (read as a stucco igloo).
- **The gizer standard (*fix*): every billet in the three piles is cut to one shortened amah (5 tefachim ⚙, ±10%), laid end to end in the rows.** Zevachim 62b: "the gizrin Moshe made — length an amah, width an amah, thickness as the strickle of a heaped se'ah; R' Yirmiyah: in a shortened amah"; Tamid 2:3 has the pile built of gizrin of fig, walnut and pine branch-wood (marbiyot); report §10.9 and Part XIV. Billets are round branch-wood of ≈ 6 cm radius, about a third split (flattened section); the two gizrin of the day (below) take the standard literally. The earlier 2–4-amah logs and the long branches across the top are gone (no longer branches are kept anywhere). Alternative: long logs.
- **The great ma'arachah (*fix*): rows of gizrin east–west, nine layers crosswise-offset with air between the rows (Tamid 2:4's kindling gaps), narrowing upward; each row runs from the staggered, broken east face (±0.45 amah — "its face east") westward until its innermost billet touches the tapuach's actual surface at that height ("the inner ends touched the tapuach"). It burns from the inside: a bed of coals and ash 0.55 amah high under its heart (toward the tapuach) swallows the lower layers; the heart is a hollow over the coals (layers 0–4); round it the billets are charred, shortened and tapered toward the heart, a third slumped 10–25° into the bed; charred and fresh mix further out; the outer faces are fresh.** Hidden interior billets are not emitted. Alternative: a tidy stack of fresh logs (read as a lumberyard).
- **Charred wood and charcoal take `timber_charred` once the materials task adds it; meanwhile `glass_dark` (charcoal's satin black), never the fresh bark.** The coal beds stay `ash` until `embers` exists (requested); at night fx's own glowing bed shows through the hollow heart. Alternative: `timber_rough` (hid the burnt core).
- **The incense pile: a small crib of thin fig gizrin (two to a row) over a heap of coals; the third pile (kiyum ha'esh) a larger crib (three to a row). Both at site.js's boxes.** Tamid 2:5 ("fine fig wood"); Yoma 45a (R' Yosi); the third pile's position is ⚙ in site.js. Alternative: loose heaps.
- **Fires (*fix*): the great pile has two `pyre` emitters 2.7 amot (1.3 m) apart — `altar.maarachah.great` (2.4 m, over the pile's eastern half) and `…great.inner` (1.9 m, in the hollow heart) — so fx clusters them into ONE light (MERGE 1.6 m), which takes the fire-shadow slot on high. Both smoke, so the great column is two overlapping fire-lit columns, wider and denser than the small piles'. Incense pile 0.75 m at intensity 0.6 (a bed of coals with low flames), third pile 1.15 m at 0.75; all `schedule: 'always'`.** Before, the inner emitter sat 2.1 m away, became its own unshadowed light and lit the north yesod and pavement through 9 amot of altar. A custom fx smoke column was tried and rejected: it carries no fire glow and rendered black at night. It is an ordinary wood fire with no supernatural glow (§32). Alternative: one emitter of size 3.
- **Ash drifts on the walkway (*fix*): four low drifts (≤ 3 cm) straddling the ma'arachah line beside the great pile (east) and the incense pile (west), their rims buried under the walkway and the floor ash.** Ash raked from the piles spills onto the kohanim's walkway between cleanings. Alternative: a clean walkway.
- **`altar.two-logs` (*fix*): the two gizrei etzim of the day (Yoma 2:5, 26b; 22a) as Moshe's standard read literally — riven boards one shortened amah long and wide and ≈ 2 etzba'ot thick (⚙ for "the strickle of a heaped se'ah"), laid on the great pile's east end; `timber_split` requested for the riven faces, `wood_lattice` meanwhile.** Zevachim 62b. Alternative: round logs.

## The kevesh and its satellites
- **Ground piece + the 2-amah flying span exactly as site.js gives them: underside +17 over the yesod line and a hair above the sovev, with a 1 cm "hair's breadth" (⚙, `A.HAIR`) before the altar on both steps of the end.** Zevachim 62b. Nothing is extruded to the floor under the flying span: there is a 1-amah slot under it at the yesod line. *fix:* nothing on the altar projects into the gap (the karkov band breaks across it; the faces there waver inward only).
- **The small ramps are solid to the floor, at site.js's boxes; their faces buried against the kevesh are not skinned.** The sovev branch therefore shows a 7½-amah end face on the south, where it leaves the kevesh (raised with site-model). Alternative: a flying branch on piers (not in site.js).
- **Revuvah: the 1 × 1 window from site.js, opening into a cavity 1½ amot deep (⚙) in the same limed mass.** Middot 3:3. The depth is unsourced.
- **Marble "table of the fats": a thick slab on two marble supports set in from the ends. Silver table: a rimmed top over a frieze, on four turned legs with stretchers; two silver bowls and a jug on it at detail ≥ 2.** Shekalim 6:4; Rambam BhB 2:15. Forms ⚙, after period furniture. *fix:* the marble slabs here and in the slaughter zone are closed underneath by the same ring as their sides (an inset underside panel left a slit of sky).
- **The silver machtah (a square pan with a handle) lies in the 1-amah slot between the yesod branch and the altar at `altar.machtah-niche`; movable `altar.machtah`.** Tamid 1:4.
- **Beit HaDeshen: a low irregular ash heap in site.js's 2 × 2 box, slightly sunk into the pavement ("swallowed in its place").** Tamid 1:4.

## Slaughter zone
- **Rings: iron, lying folded flat toward the south on a hinge-staple with a leaded iron footing at their north edge; inner radius about 0.27 amah.** Middot 3:5. The form is ⚙ (the commentators describe rings that fold up to lock the neck). Alternative: upright rings.
- **Marble tables: a slab with a raised lip round a shallow dished top (for rinsing, Rambam BhB 5:14), on two marble supports.** Form ⚙. Placed at site.js's tables band, one row.
- **Nanasin: marble round shafts (material unstated in the Mishnah) on a square plinth with a torus base, the squared cedar block 1.3 amot wide on top, closed underneath (*fix*: its inset underside left a 1 cm slit of sky).** Middot 3:5.
- **The three rows of iron hooks are three working heights (*fix*): the lamb is hung by a hind leg pierced at the hock and flayed downward (Tamid 4:2), so its hook must be within a standing kohen's reach (≈ 2 m = 4.2 amot ⚙). While the cedar block's middle is within reach, all three rows go in the block; with site.js's 5-amah shafts (NANAS_H ⚙, block at 2.4–2.9 m) only the top row — for the heavy carcasses — is in the block, and the other two are leaded into the shaft at ≈ 1.9 m and ≈ 1.6 m. Each row: two hooks toward each flaying gap (E, W), one on N and S.** Middot 3:5 / Tamid 3:5 "iron hooks were fixed in them, three rows to each" ("בהם": the pillars or their blocks); the three heights also give the common reading of rows for animals of different sizes. site-model is asked to reconsider NANAS_H ("dwarf" pillars ≈ 3 amot), which puts every row back in the block automatically. Alternative: all rows in the block at 2.5–2.85 m (out of reach).
- **Flaying stations: anchors only (`altar.flaying.1–7`).** The carcass hangs from the hooks beside the pillar. site.js does not build the "tables between the pillars" variant.

## Kiyor and muchni
- **Kiyor: a deep bronze basin on a flared stand (kiyor ve-kano), 4 × 3 from site.js, with a thick rim. Twelve spigots low on the bowl (about 0.7 m), each with a down-turned nozzle and a key, so hands and feet are washed together.** Yoma 37a (Ben Katin's twelve dadim); Tamid 3:8. Forms ⚙. Alternative: spigots at the rim.
- **It stands on a thick bronze lifting plate (rebated edge) set a hair below a dressed-stone well curb (`steps_stone`, 0.3 amah wide, 3 cm proud) over the well (site.js `params.well`) (*fix*).** The curb makes the deck read as a lifting platform rather than a mat on the pavement. Alternative: a stone deck.
- **`userData.raise(f)` sinks the whole laver, its bail and ring below the pavement (*fix*: drop 4.8 amot, was 3.5, which left the bail standing out of the floor) and turns the windlass. While the laver is down, a dark shaft mouth shows inside the curb and the rope runs into it; build-courts is asked to cut the well itself.** The muchni lowered it so its water would not be disqualified by linah (Yoma 37a–b, Abaye). Alternative: keep the laver always raised.
- **The bronze bail is hooked into the lugs only while the laver is lowered or raised (`raise(f) < 1`); once it stands in use it is unhooked and only the rope's end hangs from the sheave (*fix*).** A bail over the basin at head height framed it as a cooking cauldron on a crane. Alternative: a bail that folds onto the rim (a fold-down bail must be a semicircle of the rim's radius, whose top would reach the sheave).
- **Muchni: two weathered-timber trestles south of the kiyor (inside site.js's `params.wheel` box) carrying an E–W axle with a rope drum and a spoked hand-wheel. A boom on a cap beam reaches over the kiyor with a head sheave and is braced from the trestles' north legs; the rope runs drum → sheave → the bail.** The machine's form is unsourced; this is a period lifting machine (trestles, windlass, jib) that keeps the twelve kohanim's ring round the kiyor clear and stays above head height. *fix:* the hand-wheel stands just inside the east trestle at radius 1.7 (was 2.15, east of the drum), leaving ≥ 1.3 amot clear of the kohanim at the southern spigots; its handles face west. It uses the `wood_lattice` keys until `timber_weathered` exists. The rope uses `curtain_linen` (a fibre) until a `rope` key exists. Alternative: a gantry over the well (it would stand in the kohanim's ring and outside site.js's frame box).
- **A static cylinder collider (`altar:kiyor`) round the raised laver (*fix*).** Movables are not colliders, so the player walked through the basin; the laver is up through the day's service, and at night the same cylinder fences the shaft mouth. Alternative: ask player for movable colliders.

## Ulam steps, water, anchors
- **The twelve steps are one stepped solid across the 20-amah width (site.js profile: treads 1,1,3 ×3 then 1,1,4), with a 2 cm nosing arris; treads are the colliders.** Middot 3:6; lock #25.
- **The amah: the only water surface in courts' 1-amah-deep cut, 0.35 amah under the pavement (⚙) so it catches the light, its UVs scrolling downstream (south, then east) at 0.3 m/s; dark arched culvert mouths, crowns just under the pavement, where the shit's drain enters the head of the channel and where it goes under the pavement toward the Water Gate.** Middot 3:2 ("they mingle in the amah and go out to Nachal Kidron"). *fix:* courts no longer lays a still plane there, so the two-plane z-fight is gone. Alternative: still water only.
- **Ben Arza's station: on the Ezrat Kohanim floor at the head of the duchan, 12 amot north of the altar's south line, facing the altar.** Tamid 7:3 has the trumpeters come from the table of the fats to stand by him, and the cymbal belongs to the Levites' platform. ⚙ spot.
- **The magrefah throw spot: in the strip north of the Ulam steps (3 amot past their north edge), "between the Ulam and the altar" (Tamid 5:6).** The steps and the kiyor fill the rest of the strip. ⚙ spot.
- **Ring anchors: rows numbered from the altar northward and rings from the west; each anchor stands 1.2 amot east of its ring facing west (the shochet's place, Tamid 4:1).** `altar.ring.tamid-morning` / `-afternoon` follow site.js (NW / NE corner, second ring).
