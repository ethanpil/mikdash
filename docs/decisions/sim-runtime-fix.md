## Runtime and engineering

- **Headline cues sit at least `CUE_LEAD` = 0.5 min after their event's t0 (lib/cue.js), with the visuals tied to them.** The blasts and the first gates open at gates-blasts.t0 + 0.5, the great gate at great-gate.t0 + 0.5, the nivreshet window opens at t0 + 0.5, and the officer reaches the sleeping guard (the unanswered "Shalom alecha") at sleeping-guard.t0 + 0.5. The officer's first greeting is at ish-har-habayit.t0 + 0.5. — The UI's jump-to-event lands at t0 + 0.25 and jumps never fire cues, so a cue in [t0, t0 + 0.25] is lost to exactly the visitor who asked for that event. The sim owns the rule because the cues are the sim's; ui needs no change. Regression test: `dev/tests/jumpto.js`. — Moving the UI's landing to t0 − 0.3, which would show the scene before its event starts.
- **Every choreo is initialised at boot, and on the first frame every pooled figure stands once in front of the camera, on a ladder of distances (1.6–135 m; props on the near rungs).** — People builds each archetype × LOD batch and its material when a figure is first drawn at that LOD. The renderer then compiles the program, which took 3.4–4.7 s on the HD 4600 the first time a day-booted session reached night. Warming behind the opaque loading screen moves that cost into boot: the first night frame after a d3 boot dropped from 3,412 ms to 98–114 ms, the same as a repeat jump (`dev/tests/firstnight.js`). — Lazy init on first relevance, or a `ctx.people.precompile()` hook (requested from people; the warm frame can switch to it).
- **`sim.door(id)` is shared between choreos: each states the amount it wants in its update, the runtime applies the largest, and a door nobody wants returns to the builder's default.** — Night-watch now opens Beit HaMoked's chel door and Azarah wicket as the officer's party passes. Gates-opening owns the gates all day, and a future torch-inspection choreo will open the same wicket. Max-wins keeps each door a pure function of t across choreos, and releasing a choreo restores the default deterministically. — Each choreo writing doors directly, so the last writer wins.
- **The budget cap is `maxFigures × 4`, with crowds counted by their members. A crowd that doesn't fit is thinned with `setCount`.** — People draws `maxFigures` at LOD0–2 and up to 3× more as LOD3 silhouettes, but capping sim figures at `maxFigures` left that allowance unused. The sim's ranking (distance / importance) still decides who goes first beyond the cap. — A separate crowd share.
- **Per-slot LOD: `sim.due(slot)`. A choreo spread over the Mount skips posing far slots: 15 Hz under 350 m, 4 Hz beyond, always after a jump. Skipped slots keep their pose and visibility.** — Both reference choreos span the whole Mount, so choreo-level LOD never applied there. Night-watch's 21 posts now use it (far guard measured at 11 Hz, near at frame rate). — Per-pool bounds.
- **Per-date plans (windows, door times, cue keys, the officer's whole night) are cached on `sim.generation`, which is bumped by every `remap()`.** — No per-frame strings, closures or arrays. Greetings and door passes are found by binary search over sorted offsets from the patrol's start. `rand` takes fixed arguments, `wanderer()` draws once, `Slot.placeNear()` avoids `{ near }` literals, and per-frame runtime values live in object fields. Measured allocation per frame: day 2,703 → 321 B, night 1× 4,514 → 1,049 B, night 600× 2,213 → 1,444 B (`dev/tests/gc.js`); the remainder is mostly number boxing in un-optimised tiers. — Recomputing each frame.
- **Slot heading cache tolerance 1e-4° (was 0.05°).** — The coarse tolerance kept a stale heading that depended on the order of jumps (seen as 0.003° differences in `jumps.js`). — Keep 0.05° and round in the test.
- **The nivreshet caption is a condition cue. In continuous play inside [nivreshet.t0 + 0.5, t1 + 25 min] it fires once, when the flash first becomes visible to the observer: the sun above the env horizon profile at the nivreshet's height, a facet mirroring it to the eye (heichal.nivreshet `glint(sunDir, eye)` ≥ 0.75), and the observer within 260 m.** — build-heichal made the flash a physical reflection (Part VIII §32, no flare), so the sim follows it. On 2026-11-02 the sun clears the 2.2° profile at sunrise + 14 min, which matches build-heichal's "≈ 15 min". The cue moment is taken just inside the crossing interval (t − 1e-6), because t itself is its closed end and failed on float rounding. — A caption at the event's t0 whether or not anything flashes. The request for `ctx.fx.glint` is withdrawn.

## Determinism and the officer's timeline

- **The officer's timeline comes from the circuit's authored waypoints (straight leg lengths at 1.15 m/s, 0.35 min at each post), computed once per date. Nav only reshapes the line walked between waypoints, which is stretched over the same minutes.** — Before, the round length came from nav, which depends on the collider soup and tier. Until nav was ready (3–10 s) he was hidden, and the timeline changed when it arrived. Now he walks the authored line from boot and the timeline never changes. All nav legs are within about 6% of the authored line (two legs, both on the open Mount). — A baked table of nav lengths.
- **Two time scales close to 1 (measured ×1.051 and ×1.005): s1 so he reaches the sleeping guard exactly at the incident's anchor, and s2 so his last round ends back at his gate just before his event ends.** — With the start at his gate, the incident and the end are all fixed by the schedule; nothing waits. — The old phase offset, which left him standing at his first post for about 3.3 min.
- **Guards' sway uses minutes since 20 min before the watch, not the raw clock.** — Raw t jumped at midnight (26.5°). — Unchanged.
- **The clearing men's walk uses the nominal length of its waypoints (Gazit → north side of the Ulam → north end of the Ulam steps → Ulam → wicket), so the wicket's time is fixed from boot.** — Before, it was `dishun + 3.25` until nav resolved. — Nav length.

## Research-driven staging

- **The officer comes in and leaves by the western Chuldah gate (Middot 1:3 — the Chuldah gates serve "entry and exit"). He appears in its tunnel portal at his event's start and greets its guard at t0 + 0.5. His last round ends there and he goes out as his event ends.** — A tunnel by a gate is the least jarring place for a figure to appear. It removes the 3.3-min wait at a post. — Appearing at a post or mid-path. Shushan was rejected: "for the Parah" (Middot 1:3), and visible across the Mount.
- **He goes to all 21 Levite posts, including the one behind the Beit HaKaporet. He reaches it through Beit HaMoked: up its flight, through its chel door, across the chol half, and through the wicket in the east leaf of its Azarah gate ("a small wicket through which they went in to search the Azarah", Middot 1:7). He comes back the same way, and both doors swing as the party passes ⚙.** — Middot 1:2 / Rambam BhB 8:10: "he went round every post". The Azarah's gates are locked at night, and site.js already marks the pishpash "for the night patrol". — Skipping the post (the earlier decision), for which no source was found.
- **The circuit (order ⚙) keeps to the open ways. The Azarah gate posts are reached up their own 32-step flights. The Azarah's two eastern corners are reached through the soreg's east entry. Nikanor's post is reached up through the Ezrat Nashim, whose east gate stays open. The two western corners are greeted across the soreg from just outside it (X 51): those strips of the chel are closed off by the Gazit, Etz and Delek flights, and the soreg's breaches were repaired (Middot 2:3).** — Nav ignores door leaves, so its shortest paths crossed the Azarah through shut gates (e.g. Mayim → Bechorot → Delek) or detoured 200 m east. — Nav's own shortest paths.
- **The Levites walk in from the nearest of the Mount's entry gates (Kiponus, Shushan, the two Chuldah gates; not Tadi, which "served for nothing", Middot 1:3) to reach their posts as the watch begins, and walk out the same way when it ends (all gates are open by then). The guard behind the Kaporet arrives 3 min after the locking begins, before the Azarah's gates are shut ⚙. Walks follow nav at 1.2 m/s, capped at 2.9 min. Before nav is ready (the first seconds), they stand at their posts only.** — A walk replaces the pop at the window edges, and people has no actor fade (requested). — Fading.
- **The sleepers (rovim, the Moked) wake up onto their knees in the last 0.75 min of their window. They are `kohen_offduty`, so they kneel in their own clothes, without the priestly garments.** — Tamid 1:1: they slept in their own clothes. `sit` floats (its seat is 1 amah high). — Vanishing asleep.
- **The clearing men stand ready at the Gazit's east door with the Teni and Kuz from the start of their event. They then walk without stopping and reach the wicket 1.5 min before the great gate opens ⚙.** — Tamid 3:7 describes one continuous walk: the wicket, the ta, the Heichal, the great gate. Before, there was an 8-min gap after the wicket. — Starting the walk at the event's start.
