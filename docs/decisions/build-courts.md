# build-courts — decisions

Scope: `src/world/courts/**` — Ezrat Nashim, the fifteen steps, Sha'ar Nikanor, Ezrat Yisrael / duchan / Ezrat Kohanim,
the Azarah walls, gates and flights, the six chambers, Kelim, Beit Avtinas, Beit HaNitzotz, Beit HaMoked and the
underground route. Every position and dimension is read from `src/data/site.js`; the bullets below are the choices the
sources leave open. Format: **choice** — why — alternative. Entries revised by the review fix (build-courts-fix) are
marked (fix).

## Method
- **Walls, floors and chamber shells are greedy-meshed box solids (union of boxes minus openings/voids/cutouts/replaced spans read from each wall's params).** — one closed, outward-wound mesh per material with no internal faces; colliders come out watertight and the site's `openings / voids / replacedBy / cutouts / alsoPierces` lists map one-to-one onto the subtracted boxes — hand-modelled walls per span.
- **The courts are solid platforms: Ezrat Nashim, the Ezrat Yisrael block, the Azarah and the two podiums are filled from the Mount (Z 0) up to their floors; cellars, cisterns, mikvaot and the stair shaft are carved out of the fill.** — otherwise the Mount pavement under the courts is a standable floor (the player spawned under Ezrat Nashim); the hollow vaults under the Azarah (Zevachim 58a) are not visible anywhere — modelling the kippin.
- **Faces are dressed by where they look: Herodian drafted ashlar (`herodian_wall`) on faces toward the Mount, plain dressed coursing (`herodian_wall_inner`) toward the courts, `ashlar_plain` / `plaster` in rooms, marble (`marble_white`) in gate passages and thresholds (gatehouses of marble — Yoma 31b).** — Part VII §21 #1, #8, #10 — one material per wall.
- **All doors are instanced leaves animated through `ctx.movables` (`door:<element id>`, `userData.open(f)`, 0 = shut, 1 = folded back); cedar doors share one canonical leaf per height class, scaled per instance: 5 × 20 (four battens) for the EN corner chambers' 20-amah doors, 4 × 16 (three battens) for the rest.** (fix) — one draw call per leaf family; one 4 × 16 leaf stretched to 20 amot made the EN doors read as tall planks — one leaf set per door size.

## Walls and gates
- **Cavetto crowning cornice (≈1.2 amah) on every wall top, both faces; a smaller one on the EN corner-chamber walls; on every tier (a few thousand triangles in the marble draw call).** — the cavetto is the Second-Temple Jerusalem cornice (cf. the Kidron-valley monuments); restrained, no battlements, no machicolations, no blind arcades (Part VII §25) — a plain coping.
- **Alternate courses set out 1½ etzba'ot on the outer faces (detail ≥ 2), aligned with the 1.1 m texture courses.** — "apik shefa v'a'eil shefa" (Bava Batra 4a); the texture carries it at a distance, the geometry gives the stepped silhouette at corners and jambs up close — texture only.
- **Gate door-cases: a three-fascia marble architrave round each gate (jambs + lintel = the shekufah, Middot 2:3), a small cavetto hood at detail ≥ 2; Nikanor's case a little wider.** — period-plausible, restrained — bare openings.
- **Chamber doors, the Nikanor wickets, the Gazit doors and the Nitzotz turret door get the same marble door-case at a narrower band (0.45–0.8 amah) on their court or chel faces.** (fix) — bare cuts beside framed gates read unfinished; the wickets read as loose slabs — leave plain.
- **F5 windows: one high window (2½ × 5 amot, sill at court floor + 28) in each bay between the N and S gates, two in the W wall, with plain marble surrounds.** — Zevachim 56a attests windows in the Azarah walls without places or counts; set high, above every chamber roof, sized as windows, not slits — no windows (silence of the plan).
- **The gate leaves hinge on the outer face and fold back into the 5-amah passage (leaf width = half the 10-amah gate); the Moked gate folds into the hall's gate bay; Nitzotz's into its exedra.** — the open leaves lie against the passage walls exactly — leaves opening outward.
- **Gate leaves: gold plate over panelled wood, four panels a leaf; from detail 1 the stiles and rails stand 0.14 amah proud with a chamfer down to each sunk panel, nail-head rows at detail ≥ 2. Nikanor's: cast Corinthian bronze, the same panelling with a domed boss (with a fillet at its foot, 0.15–0.3 amah high) in each panel, unplated (lock #14).** (fix) — Middot 2:3; Yoma 38a; the earlier 3 cm frames and flat disc bosses read in raking light as thin rings and crescents; Chabad's "intricate designs" are uncited and not used — rosettes.
- **Gate leaves are as high as the opening they fold into: 20 amot, except the Water Gate's (19.45, under its ½-amah marble roof) and Nitzotz's (19.35, under the exedra's roof slab); all gold leaves scale one canonical 4.98 × 20 leaf.** (fix) — 20-amah leaves there rose through the roof and their gilded tops z-fought with the terrace floor at +36 (the striated green band at Beit Avtinas' threshold) and with Beit HaNitzotz's floor — lower gate heads (site.js gives 20).
- **Nikanor's leaves open to 72°, not flat against the passage walls (`maxAngle`; `open(1)` is that angle), so their faces show from Ezrat Nashim and the 15 steps; 6.9 amot stay clear between them.** (fix) — the sim opens every gate fully all day; folded flat the famous doors were edge-on — sim-side amounts.
- **Nikanor's wickets: single bronze leaves (as the gate), hinged at the EN face, opening into the Pinchas / chavitin chambers.** — EK/Chanukat HaBayit: the wickets are those chambers' doorways (site.js) — cedar.
- **Gate curtains (seven of the thirteen parochot, Yoma 54a): one per Azarah gate on the court face (Nikanor: on its Azarah face), on a rod 1 amah wider than the gate each side, gathered at the jambs by default; Nitzotz's hangs on its chel face because its leaves fold into the exedra.** — the sources give the count, not the hanging — curtains inside the passages (they would foul the folded leaves).
- **Chamber doors are 10 wide where the chamber allows, 16 high (EN corner chambers: the site's 20); a height given in site.js (`door.z` / `doors[].z`) is used when present.** (fix) — Middot 2:3's "all the openings there were 20 high and 10 wide" would cut through the six chambers' 20-amah roofs (CHAMBER_ROOF), so site-model is asked to raise those roofs or record the heights; until then 16 — 20-high doors through the roof.
- **The Gazit's east door is 9 wide (y 372–381), one amah short of the site's 10 (371–381).** — the site's door starts at the chamber's SE corner, which would leave the door without a south jamb; site-model is asked to move it (e.g. 371.5–381.5) or record 9 — a jambless corner opening.
- **Flights: 32 half-steps as the site gives them, with cheek walls ¾ amah thick inside the 10-amah width; their coping runs 2 amot above the nosing line at the stair's pitch, level for ¾ amah at the head and ½ amah at the foot.** (fix) — a 16-amah drop needs a parapet; one step of the cheek per four treads read as saw-teeth (merlons) against the walls, which Part VII §25 rules out — stepped cheeks / cheeks outside the width.
- **The corners where the low east walls (+28, +35½) meet the high N/S walls stay as the site gives them (the Azarah E wall's corner piers at +35½).** — site.js extends the E wall over the corners; noted for site-model — raising the corner piers.

## Ezrat Nashim
- **The gezuztra is timber (cedar): a deck on joists, a wall ledger, knee braces to bearing blocks at +12, a 2-amah parapet of close-set slats on posts (boarded parapet at detail 0); walking surface at the site's floorZ +16.** — Raavad (Part VII §21 #5) reads it as wooden; it lies outside the Azarah, so Rambam BhB 1:9's ban on wooden exedras does not apply; the element box's "1 + 2" is read as joist depth below the floor + 2-amah parapet — stone corbelled balconies.
- **Four timber stairs to the gallery (½ × ½, 20 risers), along the corner-chamber walls, clear of their doors: N and S segments from the west, the two E segments from the west along the chambers' walls; the parapet is left open where each lands, the handrail on the free side.** — access is unsourced (site.js); every segment needs one; ½ × ½ is the house's step (Middot 2:3) — stairs from outside the court.
- **Corner chambers: cedar door leaves; uses shown: log piles and scattered logs for sorting (NE), a stepped plastered mikveh with 3 amot of water and benches (NW), a stone hearth bench with three cauldrons (SE), rows of flat-based storage jars and jugs and a counter (SW).** — Middot 2:5; Yoma 31a (mikveh size); forms of jars and pots after period types ⚙ — empty courts.
- **Logs are round: one instanced 8–10-sided, slightly tapered log (bark sides in `timber_rough` at 1.8× UV density, so the plates suit 15-cm logs; each sawn end mapped onto one whole, unsplit log end of the `timber_pile_end` texture, found by re-running the material generator's block layout and PCG hash on the CPU), rolled at random about its axis. From detail 1 each pile's exposed long face and top are courses of these logs over a box core; detail 0 keeps the box pile. The same logs make the scattered sorting logs and the star fires of the Moked hearth, the Beit HaTevilah medurah and the nazirites' hearth.** (fix) — square beams and brick-like pile faces were the most obvious CG tell at 2–5 m; one draw call per material group — modelled piles throughout.
- **The nazirites' cauldrons stand on three hearth stones each (pot base 0.95 amah over the bench), the fire beneath the middle one among sticks; it burns by day (`schedule: 'day'`).** (fix) — the fire sat inside the pot; they cooked their shelamim when they brought them, by day — an open pit beside the pots; burning all night.
- **One paving block in the wood chamber is a different (veined) marble.** — Shekalim 6:2 (the kohen who noticed "a paving block different from its fellows" — the tradition of the hidden Aron); a quiet detail, 5 mm proud — omit.
- **The fifteen steps are true half-annuli about the Nikanor wall face (riser normals smooth), 24–96 segments by tier, closed underneath by a down-facing half-disc a hair above the pavement; treads are the colliders (½-amah risers ≤ 0.35 m).** (fix) — Middot 2:5 "like half a round threshing floor"; an open tread shell left the +6 pavement under the steps standable, so a go-to onto the steps landed inside them — a polygonal approximation with ramps.
- **Music cellars: cedar racks with lyres and harps (simple soundbox-and-arms forms, detail ≥ 1) along both long walls; the rack on the door wall stops an amah short of the doorway; cedar door leaves opening in.** (fix: the door-wall rack ran across the doorway) — Middot 2:6; instrument forms ⚙ — empty rooms.
- **Lishkat Chasha'im: plastered room, roof just below the gallery deck, a chest and a table.** — Shekalim 5:6 (secret gifts) — —

## Azarah structures
- **Water Gate house: walls ½ amah (thin marble gatehouse, Yoma 31b), side rooms with doors off the passage; the stair in the east room enlarged to the room's full depth (r 1.95; site box 3 × 4) and its last step turned onto the terrace walk.** — a 3-amah stair cannot pass a person (the player's capsule is 0.6 m); access is unsourced — a straight stair (does not fit 20 amot of rise).
- **Newel stairs (Water Gate, Nitzotz, Medichin, the Moked descent): solid stone steps round a newel of r ½ amah, each step's underside a helical soffit half an amah below its nosing line; sized for a person — Nitzotz r 2.0, Medichin r 1.9 (a little past the site's 4 × 4 box including walls), Water Gate r 1.95, the Moked descent r 1.95 — leaving ≥ 1.35 amot of tread and ≥ 5 amot of head room.** (fix) — a mesibah is a stone newel stair; the earlier 0.25-amah newel and floating wedges read as a modern corkscrew — the site boxes as given (impassable).
- **The Nitzotz stair stands in a thin (½-amah) stone turret in the exedra's NE corner, entered by a framed doorway in its south face; the first step rises just inside it and three full turns bring the top step back over it, opening onto Beit HaNitzotz's floor.** (fix) — a free-standing helix in the open exedra was not period-plausible and was seen from the whole Azarah; Middot 1:5 gives the upper storey, not the stair — the stair outside the exedra.
- **The KG's first mikveh tank is narrowed to 3½ × 2½ (site: 4 × 4) so a 1.6-amah walk runs along the court edge from the stair to Beit Avtinas; water stands 3 amot over its floor at gate head + 3 (Yoma 31a); steps up its west side and down inside.** (fix: the size was misprinted as 4 × 2½) — with the full 4 × 4 tank the terrace stair could not reach Beit Avtinas; the tank's size is ⚙ in site.js — move Beit Avtinas.
- **The Kohen Gadol's linen sheets (Yoma 3:4; site `params.screen`) — on the Water Gate roof over the court-side parapet beside the mikveh, and on the Parvah roof along the tank's north side — are movables (`curtain:azarah.mikveh.mayim-roof.screen`, `curtain:azarah.chamber.parvah.screen`), rolled to their cedar poles by default (`open(1)`) and spread with `open(0)` for his Yom Kippur immersions.** (fix) — the sheet was spread only then; the Parvah one had stood spread permanently and the Water Gate one was missing — dropping the sheets.
- **Beit Avtinas: a narrow plastered room (side walls 0.6), a cedar door leaf at its east end onto the walk (`door:azarah.beit-avtinas`), three windows toward the court (the kohanic watch), a long worktable with stone mortars and spice jars.** (fix: leaf) — Tamid 1:1; Yoma 38a (compounding); Middot 2:3 (every opening there had doors) — no windows.
- **Nitzotz exedra: open to the court through four marble columns (symmetric about the gate axis) under a marble architrave; the gate in its back wall; Beit HaNitzotz with four open bays over the court above a 2-amah sill.** — Middot 1:5 ("like an exedra … an upper storey built on it"); Rambam BhB 1:9 (exedras in the Azarah only of stone); F12 — closed rooms.
- **Columns: marble, Attic base, slight entasis, echinus-and-square-abacus capital (Tuscan-like).** — restrained and period-plausible; Corinthian is attested in Herodian Jerusalem but would be decorative invention here — Corinthian capitals.
- **The amah channel is cut into the Azarah pavement (1 × 1) along site.js's open segments; its running water surface is build-altar's (`altar/water.js`).** (fix: the courts' own still plane 0.04 amah under altar's surface was removed — it risked z-fighting) — it lies in this builder's pavement — leave the cut to build-altar.

## The six chambers
- **Gazit: a basilica — the kodesh half a nave between two rows of three marble columns under stone architraves (no wood in the Azarah — Rambam BhB 1:9), the chol half an open hall under cedar beams for the Sanhedrin; rashei pispasin heads in the floor on the dividing line.** — Yoma 25a ("like a great basilica, half in kodesh, half in chol"); Middot 1:6 (rashei pispasin as the marker) — columns throughout.
- **The Sanhedrin's place: four stepped semicircular tiers of marble benches (radii 4.6–8.95, rise 0.6) open toward the chol door, ≈ 85 places at an amah each, the nasi's seat raised at the apex; the two scribes' places are standing lecterns (sloped desks on a post, top ≈ 2.1 amot) before them, right and left.** (fix: lecterns) — Sanhedrin 4:3 ("like half a round threshing floor so they see one another"; "two scribes of the judges stand before them"); EK's "a man sits in an amah" (Part X §52) shows one row of 71 cannot fit a 20-wide hall; tiers solve it — sitting desks.
- **96 garment niches (Tamid 5:3: "the uses of the vessels" written on them; four per mishmar) in two rows along both long walls of the kodesh half (detail ≥ 1).** — the commentators' four per mishmar × 24 — none.
- **Gazit windows high in the free south wall and the chol north wall; four lamp-stands lit at night.** — chambers' windows are unattested (F-table), but a 45-amah hall needs light; the dawn lotteries were held before daylight — a dark hall.
- **Golah: the cistern carved under the floor (site box), a 3 × 3 mouth with a marble curb, two posts carrying an axle with a rope drum and a large spoked hand-wheel (`wheel:azarah.chamber.golah`), a rope and copper bucket.** — Middot 5:4 ("the wheel placed on it"); the machine's form ⚙ — a pulley wheel only.
- **Etz/Parhedrin: a plastered dwelling with a cedar ceiling; the bed runs north–south; a table, chest, lamp, a wall shelf with jugs and pegs with a linen mantle (detail ≥ 1); one door north to the chel with its flight, its leaves hung half an amah inside the chel face.** (fix: bed, shelf) — Rambam BhB 7:9 (from Berachot 5b): one sleeps only between north and south, never east–west; Yoma 1:1 (his seven days there); Rambam BhB 6:7 (opens only to chol) — an east–west bed.
- **The mezuzah: a slim cedar case on the face of the west jamb (the right doorpost of one entering from the chel), proud into the opening, in the doorway's outer tefach, from the start of the upper third (detail ≥ 1).** (fix: it had been modelled inside the masonry) — Yoma 10a–11a (the only chamber with a mezuzah); Menachot 33a–b (upper third; the outer tefach) — no case (a recess).
- **Parvah roof: the KG's Yom-Kippur mikveh as a raised tank (water at +39, 3 amot deep), steps up its west side, his linen sheet on poles along its north side (a movable, see above), parapets round the Parvah–Medichin roof; the Medichin stair arrives on the Medichin roof beside it.** — Middot 5:3; Yoma 3:4, 31a — a sunken pool (not possible over a 20-amah room without a deeper roof).
- **Salt in low heaps behind kerbs (Melach), hides hanging over cedar poles in two flaps with flayed-hide outlines (Parvah; `leather` requested, `olive_wood` meanwhile), marble rinsing tables and a water trough (Medichin).** (fix: hide shapes) — Middot 5:3 uses — empty rooms.

## Beit HaMoked and the underground
- **Hall walls to the springing (+36) and a 1-amah course over them; corner slabs carry a hemispherical dome (inner r 9½, outer r 10 — crown at the site box top) over the 20 × 20 square; an oculus (r 1.1) as a vent.** — Middot 1:6 / Tamid 1:1 "kippah"; a fire burning indoors needs a vent; pendentives would push the crown above the site box — a closed dome / pendentive dome.
- **Carved rings inside the dome at detail ≥ 2.** — Herodian domes carry geometric relief (cf. the Double Gate vestibule); kept to three mouldings — plain plaster.
- **Rovadin: three ledges of ½ rise and 1 amah depth along the E and W walls between the corner chambers (the only free wall stretches); elders sleep on the chol half of them (Yoma 25a), rashei pispasin across the hall at the site's kodesh line.** — Middot 1:8 "ringed with rovadin of stone"; the site's corner chambers leave only these stretches — ledges round every face (would close the gate bays).
- **The hearth: a stone kerb with embers and a star fire of round logs in the chol half, 5 amot north of the kodesh line (between the north corner chambers, before the door to the chel), clear of the keys slab.** (fix) — everyone it serves sits or sleeps in the chol half (no sitting in the Azarah — Rambam BhB 7:6; Yoma 25a; the pirchei kehunah "each with his cover on the ground" — Tamid 1:1); site.js says "centre of the hall", which is the kodesh line and the keys slab (told to site-model) — in the kodesh half by the slab.
- **The kohanic watch-post lamp stands before the west ledges just north of the kodesh line, more than 3 amot from every corner-chamber door.** (fix) — it had stood in the NW chamber's doorway, blocking the only way to the stair — on a ledge.
- **The hall's interior and its corner chambers are dressed limestone (`ashlar_plain`), not the banded court marble.** — a service hall lit by its hearth; the banded marble stays on the court and chel faces — banded marble inside too.
- **Corner chambers: two thin inner walls and a roof slab each, the door at the corridor end of the wall (clear of the ledges), each with a cedar leaf (`door:azarah.moked.chamber.<key>`): the lamb chamber's opens in through its splayed corner (Yoma 17a "cut off at the corner"); the other three open out into the hall and fold back against the ledges' ends; a manger, Beit Garmu's domed oven and table, a heap of whitewashed altar stones; the NW chamber holds the stair shaft.** (fix: leaves) — Middot 1:6; Middot 2:3 (every opening had doors); Yoma 38a; lock #8; opening out keeps the leaves off the oven, the stones and the stair — square chambers / leaves opening in.
- **The pishpash is a second leaf hinged inside the Moked gate's east leaf (`door:azarah.gate.moked.pishpash`), opening into the hall; the gate defaults shut, the wicket shut.** — Middot 1:7 (R. Yehudah); site.js places it in the east leaf — a separate door.
- **Underground: the winding stair (56 half-steps, four full turns, ascending clockwise) whose head is a marble landing step widened round the south of the shaft to the NW chamber's door (the floor bridges the last 0.9 amah; a parapet on the landing's open edge; its underside thins toward its far end for head room), a barrel-vaulted passage (springing 2 amot below the crown) with lamp niches every 4 amot on both sides, the Beit HaTevilah plastered, its mikveh carved as five steps down from the east (3 amot of water), the medurah as a stone fire-ring with a star fire of logs and benches, the privy of honour a small plastered room with a door.** (fix: the head of the stair) — Tamid 1:1; Middot 1:9; the stair's top step had been on the far side of the shaft from the door — a straight stair.
- **The underground masonry stops 1.2 amot under the Mount pavement.** — the pavement (build-mount) is a single-sided quad layer; the shaft needs a hole in it (requested).

## Lamps and fires
- **Watch-post lamps (schedule 'night'): stone lamp-stands at the kohanic posts (Beit Avtinas, Beit HaNitzotz, Beit HaMoked) and the Levite posts at Nikanor, the Azarah corners and behind the Beit HaKaporet; small stone brackets on the outer wall faces beside the N/S gates at their flights' heads.** — positions from `mount.watch-posts`; the fittings ⚙ — torches (the patrol carried torches — Middot 1:2 — so the posts get the quieter light).
- **Lamps are knife-pared Herodian oil lamps (instanced); `terracotta` requested, `terrain_soil` meanwhile.** — the period's common lamp — bronze lamps.
