## Research-driven

- **The bound tamid ('bound') lies on its RIGHT side, fore- and hind-leg drawn together, neck stretched back** — Tamid 4:1 (sources/Mishnah Tamid): "they did not tie (kofet) but bound it as an akedah, and those who won the limbs held it; its head to the south and its face to the west; the slaughterer stands in the east facing west". Gemara Tamid 31b / Bartenura: akedah = "the hand with the foot, like the binding of Yitzchak"; Rambam Temidin uMusafin 1:10: not tied at all, "they held its fore and hind feet by hand". Right side down puts the throat toward the slaughterer in the east when the actor's heading is 180 (head south); the drawn-back head turns the face west. Default = held (Rambam); `bound_tied` shows a cord (Bartenura's reading). Bartenura: head south so dung does not fall near the altar. — left side down (throat away from the slaughterer).
- **Hung lamb hangs by the LEFT hind hock** — Tamid 4:2: "he did not break the leg but pierced the hock and hung it"; the right hind leg is cut off while it hangs and the left taken last ("נטל רגל השמאלי"), so it hung by the left. — both hind legs.
- **Carcass states as free props with their origin at the hook**: `lamb_hung` (slaughtered, fleece on), `carcass_flaying` ("flays downward until he reaches the breast" — the hide turned down round the forequarters, head still on), `carcass_hung` (flayed, head and lower legs off, opened), `hide`. The sim places them at the nanasin hooks with an exact Z. — skinned states on the animal rig (would need per-vertex state morphs).
- **Tamid 4:3 limb set, carried ON THE PALM ('palm' grip, pose `carry_limbs`)** — the Mishnah's placements: head in the right hand, nose toward the arm, horns between the fingers, slaughter cut up with the fat (peder) on it (so it lies forehead-down on the open palm); "skin side outward" for legs and flanks; the alyah "hangs between his fingers"; the neck's ribs "between his fingers"; 6th the entrails in a bazach with the lower legs on top. `ctx.people.tamid.carriers` lists the nine pairs in order. — fists (a fist cannot hold a head "with the horns between the fingers").
- **Limbs are flayed (fell and fat outside, marbled cut faces)** — an olah is flayed (Vayikra 1:6). ~~the head too~~ — superseded: the head is NOT flayed (Tamid 4:2 cuts it off when the flaying reaches the breast); see `people-realism-fix.md`.
- **The yearling tamid lamb has short horns** — Tamid 4:3 "its horns between his fingers". Rams (second year) carry full spirals. — polled lambs.
- **Sheep breed: fat-tailed, white coarse fleece in clumped locks, brown-to-black short-haired face and legs, long pendulous ears, convex nose (Awassi type)** — the alyah (Vayikra 3:9; Tamid 4:3) requires a fat-tailed breed; colours and ears **reconstruction** after the region's breed. Goat: black long-haired Levantine; bull: red-brown, darker head and neck, dewlap — **reconstruction**.
- **Male animals are visibly male (a small scrotum; no sheath)** — the offerings here are male (tamid lamb, ram, se'ir, par); Tamid 4:3 mentions the two testicles with the right leg. Kept modest. — sexless bodies.
- **Birds: turtledove (tor) and young pigeon (ben yonah) as separate kinds, in pairs in a wicker cage (`bird_cage`, `bird_cage_pigeons`), `dove_held` for melikah** — Vayikra 1:14, 5:7, 12:8; melikah Vayikra 1:15. Plumage after the living species. — one generic dove.
- **Avnet: 3 etzba'ot (AMAH/8) wide, ~1.5 cm of windings, embroidery covering about a third of its face** — Rambam Klei HaMikdash 8:19 ("winding upon winding", 3 fingers × 32 amot), 8:1 (only the avnet is embroidered with wool), 8:15 (rokem shows on one face). The design itself (diamonds between argaman rails) is **reconstruction**. — the 8 cm all-linen band (old look).
- **Migba'at wound "like a hat" in steep diagonal bands; mitznefet wound "like a bandage" in near-level bands** — Rambam KhM 8:2. — horizontal rings.
- **Ketonet hangs to just above the heel, sleeves hand-wide to the wrist, blousing a little at the avnet** — Rambam KhM 8:16–17, 8:4 ("new, attractive, meshulshalim"). — unchanged.
- **Mantle = a rectangle laid over both shoulders: front panels and back to the shin, the sides over the upper arms to the elbow; tzitzit at the inner and outer bottom corners of the front panels; 8 ends with one techelet strand and a wound gedil** — tzitzit Bamidbar 15:38, Rambam Tzitzit 1:6–7 (one strand of eight techelet; the winding). How the tallit was draped is **reconstruction** (shawl drape). — a cone/cape (old look); the asymmetric Greek himation.
- **Sleeping kohanim covered by their own mantle** — Tamid 1:1 "they covered themselves with their own clothes" (the old `people.md` decision already switches them to their own clothes). The cover is part of the `folded_garments` pillow variants.
- **Offering poses** (`clips.js` OFFERING_CLIPS): semichah with both hands on the head (Menachot 9:8 "with all his strength"; `semichah_high` for a bull), `hold_legs` (Tamid 4:1), `receive` (kabbalah), `flay` (Tamid 4:2), `lead`/`lead_walk` (Tamid 3:5 "draws it"), `wave` — to and fro, then up and down (Menachot 61a), `kemitzah` (three fingers over the palm, Menachot 11a), `melikah` (Vayikra 1:15), `stoke` with the tzinorah fork (Tamid 1:4), `toss`, `salt` (Tamid 4:3 "and they salted them"). Exact gestures are **reconstruction**.
- **Meal-offering pans: machavat flat, marcheshet deep with a lid** — Menachot 5:8 (R' Chanina b. Gamliel: deep/flat; R' Yosi HaGelili: lid). Both of copper — **reconstruction**.
- **Shechitah knife: long, straight, square-ended iron blade** — iron is used off the altar (report §21 materials); the form is **reconstruction**; `knife_flay` is shorter and pointed.
- **Kinnor and nevel after the Judean coin lyres** (box chest + arms + yoke; jar-shaped nevel), 7 / 10 strings (Arachin 13b; Tehillim 33:2) — **reconstruction** of form.

## Engineering

- **Skirt cage instead of a second weight set** — 4 extra bones (MAX_BONES 32 → 36) solved after every baked frame from the legs: k = max(thigh flexion vs pelvis, thigh vs world vertical, shin vs vertical); upper panel = slerp(half-thigh, thigh, k), hem = slerp(near-vertical, shin, k). Zero runtime cost, works for crossfades and keyed one-shots, no extra vertex attributes (the HD 4600 has 16; we use 15). — dual weight sets blended per instance (2 more attributes and 8 bone fetches).
- **Per-instance morphs from one attribute** — `pMorph` (xyz face-shape field, w fullness along the normal) × `pAnimB.y/z` (free instance slots). Fields are spatial so skin, eyes, lids and beard shells move together; arms are excluded. — per-figure geometry (one batch each).
- **Eyes modelled only at LOD0** (eyeball + lid shells in a deep socket); LOD1 keeps painted eyes (pattern `FACE1`). Supersedes `people.md`'s "no modelled eyes": at 1.5–3 m painted eyes read as a mask; with lids covering the iris top the gaze is calm, not staring. — painted eyes everywhere.
- **Lighting extras only for the sun/moon** — `lights_fragment_begin` is spliced so the directional and SunLight loops call `RE_Direct_People` (wrap + translucency); fire point lights keep plain physical shading (every light's RE_Direct is inlined into the program).
- **No Kajiya–Kay term** — measured on the HD 4600, medium, debug scene: 31.7 fps without it, 18.2 with it. Hair gets an anisotropic-looking sheen from strand-aligned bump ridges with lower roughness instead (free). — per-light strand specular.
- **Pattern code in groups compiled per program** (`FIG`, `KG`, `BEAST`, `PROP`, `FLESH` defines) and **no derivatives inside pattern branches** — gradients in flow control make D3D's compiler flatten every branch: one kohen program took 15–18 s on ANGLE; now ~5–7 s, a lamb ~3 s. — one big uber-shader.
- **Heroes cast shadows through an LOD1 proxy** — an LOD1 instance in a batch whose colour pass draws nothing (vertices sent outside the clip volume) but whose depth material casts; LOD0 batches cast none. Each shadow pass (2 sun cascades on high, 6 faces per torch shadow) then draws ~3k instead of ~16–20k triangles per hero. — LOD0 shadows.
- **Carcass and bird props are baked from the live rigs on the CPU** (pose → skinning matrices → vertices, palette baked into vertex colours) so the hung lamb and the caged birds match the living animals exactly. — separate hand-made meshes.
- **Lead ropes: one dynamic mesh for every rope** (one draw call), a sagging 5-sided tube from the halter's chin ring to the leader's fist, rebuilt per frame from the baked bone matrices. — static ropes (wrong when either figure moves).
- **Animal optional parts by mask groups**: halter (bit 3) and its hanging lead end (bit 4) shown by the led poses (the end hides when a leader holds the rope), the akedah cord (bit 5) by `bound_tied`.
- **LOD0 budget ~14–20k triangles per human hero (KG ~29k with 144 bells/pomegranates), sheep ~11k with shells** — within the hero caps (8/14/24/48 by tier). Measured in the harness debug scene (296 figures, 214 drawn): low 49 fps (empty scene 57), medium 33.7 fps (the original material in the same scene: 36.7). People draw calls in that worst-case view: 60 meshes on medium (incl. proxies and ~25 prop variants), 40 on low.
