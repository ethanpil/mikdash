# build-mount — decision log

`- **Choice** — why (source / reasoning) — alternative.`
Scope: `src/world/terrain/**` (the land, city, trees, rocks, terraces, streets, the Parah burning place) and `src/world/mount/**`
(Har HaBayit: floor, walls, gates, Shushan HaBirah, stoas, soreg/chel, chambers, causeway, bridges, passages).
Every position and dimension is read from `src/data/site.js` (`ctx.el`); the choices below are only what the sources
leave open. Entries marked *(fix)* were revised after the accuracy / visual reviews (task build-mount-fix).

## Terrain

- **Ground = the site.js profiles, made into a seamless field by bands round the footprint**: east band `P_E(u) + T_K(Y)·b_K(u) + (1−b_K)·ΔP_N|S`, west band likewise with the Tyropoeon, south/north bands `footProfile(X) + ΔP_S|N(d)`. The ground equals every wall's `footProfile` on the wall faces (checked: max error 0.0000 amah over all four faces, the Tadi apron included), the Kidron keeps running north and south of the Mount and its bed follows the site.js thalweg line along the Mount's frontage. — radial/angle blends at the corners (they made the Kidron vanish north of the Mount); a hand-modelled DEM (not sourced).
- **Profiles interpolated with a monotone cubic (PCHIP) through every site.js point** — rounds the Kidron bed and the crest as real ground is, without overshoot; the values at the site.js points are exact. — straight segments (sharp creases at 60/200/330/480/650/800 m).
- **Tyropoeon bed falls 1.2 % to the south** ⚙ (no number in the sources; the lower city lies south). — level valley.
- **Har HaMishchah ridge meanders and dips (±70 m, ±14 m) away from the Temple axis, never on it** — the crest on the axis stays exactly 808 m (the Parah eye of site.js); a perfectly straight N–S ridge read as artificial. — uniform ridge.
- *(fix)* **The Kidron bed meanders (up to ±60 m, ≈ ±30 m typical, 420 m wavelength) north and south of the Mount, zero along its frontage** (the site.js thalweg is exact there, checked on the axis; the shift acts only on the valley, u ≈ 10–560 m, ramped so the warp stays monotone) — the straight bed read as a ruler-straight road in the aerials. — straight bed.
- *(fix)* **North and south of the Mount the rows shift sideways (±40 m, from 60 m out, zero against the walls)** ⚙ — the N/S walls' corner foot blends and the band boundaries otherwise ran on as ruler-straight N–S scarps (the "straight roads" of the aerials). — straight scarps.
- *(fix)* **Beyond the context box the profiles fade (over 1.4 km) into generic ridges and valleys**: ridge-top height by azimuth (≈800–845 m W/N/S, 690–705 m E/SE), valleys cut by ridged noise (3.4 km / 130 m and 1.4 km / 35 m), farther ridges a little higher to the west so the ridgelines layer, rising ~45 m toward the north-west and falling up to 160 m into the desert east ⚙ — the Judean ridges round the city stand ≈800–850 m with wadis 120–200 m lower; the earlier gentle hills read as a flat "lake" at dawn. (At sunrise from the crest the terrain lower than the crest's grazing ray is in its shadow and stays blue until the sun clears the crest — correct, not a defect.) Generic, not a claim about named hills. — clamp (flat), a real DEM (downloaded data, forbidden).
- *(fix)* **A skirt ring continues the field's outer edge 50 % further out, sinking 6 %** — the land never ends in a visible cut edge; it falls below the horizon into the haze. (Aerial-perspective density at the draw distance is time-env's.) — a cut edge.
- **Relief noise ±2–3 m, zero against the walls (fades in over 6–70 m) and on graded beds** — keeps the footProfile exact. — no noise (looked like plaster).
- *(fix)* **Limestone strata in the ground tint: a band of bare rock every ~7.5 m of height on moderate slopes, in patches, within ~2.5 km** ⚙ — the horizontally banded outcrops are what make Judean hills read as such (not dunes); faded out where the grid cells cannot carry them. — uniform tint.
- **Mesh: one tensor grid with fine lines near the Mount and on the east (growth length 520 m east, 260–300 m elsewhere), grid lines on every footProfile breakpoint, cut out under the footprint; lines ≈ 1.12 × / 0.9 × `quality.terrainSegments`, out to `quality.drawDistance`.** — crack-free, the footProfile is linear between grid lines. — nested LOD patches (T-junction cracks), a uniform grid.
- *(fix)* **Rendered as 2×2 / 3×3 / 4×4 tiles by grid index (low / medium / high+) so the camera can cull them; the visual ground casts no shadow — a coarse depth-only proxy grid (≈23 k triangles, `colorWrite:false`, sunk 0.6 m so it never self-shadows the visual surface) casts the land's shadows** (medium+). Cuts the high-tier shadow passes by ≈ 400 k triangles per cascade and the fire cube-shadow spikes. — one 600 k-triangle mesh in every cascade.
- **Collider = the visual grid up to 300 k triangles; above that (ultra) the high-tier grid** — keeps `player` under its 750 k collider warning. — always the visual mesh.
- **TEMPORARY ground material: the library's own `terrain_rock` maps and shader patch in a MeshStandardMaterial with `vertexColors`** (terrain/material.js), tinted per vertex from bare limestone (steep ground, karst outcrops, the Kidron bed) to grey-brown stony soil, cultivated ground in the gardens/terraces, soil on graded beds. *(fix)* normal map at 0.4 × and the grid UVs at 0.6 × (the 8 m rock tile covers ≈13 m) so the cracks stop reading as repeated decals; tints shifted from terra rossa toward grey-brown (no pink cast at dawn). Replaced automatically by a library key `terrain_blend` (requested). — per-triangle `terrain_soil`/`terrain_rock` (read as camouflage), a hard split along a contour.
- **Streets and the Parah path graded level across their width (bed under the paving), paving strips draped on the field with low skirts** — no z-fighting, no floating at low tiers. *(fix)* Strip edges mitred at bends; the Kiponus street starts exactly at the bridge deck's end and the Parah path at the causeway deck's end (no gap, no coplanar overlap). — paving as terrain material.
- *(fix)* **Graded flats carry their own offset under the walking surface**: 0.32 m under the plazas paved here (Ophel plaza, burning place), the street paving's lift (0.17 m) at the Kiponus and causeway landings so the paving meets the deck level exactly (the Kiponus west end had a 0.32 m ledge), 0 at the Tadi apron (unpaved: the ground there is exactly the north wall's `outerFootZ`). — one offset for all.

## Land use (all reconstruction — the research names the places, not a plan)

- **City districts: the upper city across the Tyropoeon (W), a north-west quarter, the Ophel / lower city (S), a sparse north; nothing east** (Kidron gardens and the olive mount). A 30 m clear belt round the Mount (Herod's esplanade is not modelled — site.js). — houses against the walls.
- *(fix)* **Houses: blocks of 2–4 × 2–3 lots, mostly party walls, one or two storeys (rarely three), flat roofs, some upper rooms (aliyot), roof parapets (ma'akeh, Devarim 22:8) from detail 2 and a plain roof-edge course; walls of dressed stone or lime-wash along the streets (seen up close: `ashlar_plain` 75 %, `plaster` 25 %), mostly rubble elsewhere (`terrain_rock` 55 %, `ashlar_plain` 35 %, `plaster` 10 % — darker from afar); roofs of rolled earth (`ash` as the stand-in for a requested `roof_earth`, 80 %) or lime (`roof_tile`)** — the bright lime roofs read as sugar cubes at the Temple's own value; earth roofs (rolled flat) are period-normal and put the city a step below the marble. Some empty lots are walled courtyards. Merged into the static batch. — all-lime roofs; rock-textured walls (stretched karst on walls).
- *(fix)* **Houses facing a street (side within 14 m of its edge) within ~420 m of the Mount get a recessed doorway (cedar door, 0.35 m reveal, a lintel stone a little proud) and one or two small high windows** — the walkable streets (Ophel, Kiponus) need doors; high small windows are the period's norm. — blank boxes.
- *(fix)* **Few streets, bending gently away from the Mount**: the Tyropoeon street along the valley floor, the street from the Kiponus bridge into the upper city, the Ophel street up to the Chuldah plaza, two cross streets. Herodian-style large paving (`paving_mount`). — ruler-straight streets (read as cuts in the aerials).
- *(fix)* **Olives planted in loose rows on the terrace benches (3.5–5 m uphill of each wall, 8.5–12 m apart, gaps), irregular groves beyond the terraces on the crest and east side; garden trees (fig, pomegranate) in two rows in the Kidron plots; a few olives north** — near/far level of detail swapped by camera distance; only the near trees cast shadows. Foliage uses `plaster` tinted per instance (silvery grey-green olives, warmer garden green) until `olive_foliage` exists (requested). — uniform scatter.
- *(fix)* **Tree form: a short leaning bole splitting into three gnarled leaders; the crown 6–7 lumps round a ring plus one over the centre, flattened, each vertex pushed in or out, its base lifted ~1.7 m** — an olive's broad, broken silhouette instead of a lollipop. Leaf-cluster cards would need an alpha texture: requested from materials (`olive_foliage_card`). — ball crowns.
- *(fix)* **Anchor view cones kept clear**: no tree, rock or terrace within 12 m of the lookouts or inside ±40° / 60 m of their view (Har HaMishchah eye, slope lookout, Kidron). — 8 m squares.

## The Parah's place (Middot 1:3, 2:4; Parah 3:6–9)

- **Burning place: a small stone floor at the crest on the axis (pyre box of site.js + 2½ amot), its beit tevilah a 4 × 4 × 3 stone tank NE of it (Parah 3:7 "a beit tevilah there"; 40 se'ah needs only 1 × 1 × 3 — Yoma 31a)** ⚙ size/position. *(fix)* The floor is dressed stone (`steps_stone`, not `bedrock`, which the library reserves for the Even Shetiyah) and its top is flush with site.js's crest ground, so the standing eye of the anchor is exactly `kohenEye` (it was 0.25 amah high). — no structure.
- **The pyre "like a tower with windows, its facade westward" (Parah 3:8) as a hidden movable `parah.pyre`** (crossed logs in courses inside the site.js box, the west face open, windows in the sides); `userData.show(bool)`. The pyre box stands between `kohenEye` and the Heichal and rises 4.7 amot above the eye; Parah 3:9 has the kohen "descend" (ירד) after sprinkling, i.e. he stood raised — the resolution is site-model's (requested); the builder keeps to the box meanwhile. — always visible.
- *(fix)* **Path from the causeway's end to the burning place: graded on the axis, rock-cut / trodden (`terrain_rock`)** (site.js: "continuesAs: path up the slope") — 29 % at its steepest; the player walks it. — a marble-looking walkway (`bedrock` read as polished slabs); switchbacks (unsourced).
- **Anchors**: `mount.har-hamishchah` at the kohen's eye of site.js facing 270, pitch −2.5° — the sight-line view; plus `mount.har-hamishchah.slope` 260 amot down the slope, where the Kidron opens below (from the eye the valley floor is hidden by the convex crest, which is what site.js's real topography implies).

## Stoas (mount.stoa.*)

- **Columns at the site.js rows (10 and 20 from the wall) with bays divided evenly between fixed points (the corners and the two columns flanking each gate passage at ±7, a 12-amah clear way for the 10-amah gate)** — no column in a gate's path; bays ≈ 10 (9.8–10.1). — a strict 10-amah grid.
- **Order: monolithic white-marble columns, shaft 14.2 amot, Ø 2 → 1.72 with slight entasis, Attic base on a square plinth, plain Doric-type capital (echinus + square abacus), no fluting, no figures or foliage** — restrained, aniconic; Corinthian capitals (Josephus's Royal Stoa) rejected as Josephus-derived and more ornate. *(fix)* Two instanced LODs on medium+: full mouldings within 90 / 120 / 150 m of the camera, an 8-sided plain version beyond. — Ionic or Corinthian.
- **Entablature: three-fascia architrave on both rows, a plain frieze and cornice on the front, all white marble; ceiling of cedar beams every 2 amot, cedar boards; flat lime roof (`roof_tile` = ma'azivah) at +20 (site.js roof top), walkable** — the itztaba roof of Shekalim 8:4 / Sukkah 4:4. *(fix)* The cedar ceiling and beams cast the stoa's shadow: three r186 renders a FrontSide material's back faces into the shadow map, so the up-facing roof could never cast and the sun passed through every stoa roof. — timber architraves.
- *(fix)* **Raised gate bays over the outer aisle before Shushan, Kiponus and Tadi**: between the columns flanking each gate, transverse marble architraves across the aisle (at the architrave level) carry side walls and an attic over the middle row; a cedar soffit above the gate head and its inner lintel (+21.4 east, +22.6 elsewhere — above Tadi's leaning stones at +22.1), the bay's roof at the low east wall's top (+21.95, still far under the Parah line) or 3.6 above the stoa roof elsewhere ⚙ — the 18.7 ceiling cut across the 20-amah gate heads and a band of sky showed through an open gate. ## Approaches

- *(fix)* **Parah causeway (context.parah-causeway): "arches upon arches, an arch over the pier" (Parah 3:6, הכפה כנגד האטום) along its whole length** — bays of 20; the upper arches are centred over the lower piers, the upper piers over the lower arches. The tier line halves the height in every bay (on a half-amah course, stepped at the upper piers under a string course), so both tiers stand wherever the arcade stands; a tier too short for a semicircle gets a segmental arch; piers thicken with their height (upper 4 → 6.5, lower 6 → 9 amot). Only where the slope comes within 12 amot of the deck at the east end (the last 50 amot ≈ 24 m) does it close in a solid abutment. The lower tier is ½ amah wider each side; deck paved between 1-amah parapets of 10 tefachim (site.js `parapet: 1` read as thickness) ⚙. — a fixed tier line at −50 (left the eastern ~80 m single-tiered: the deck stood on solid masonry from the ground, the very thing the double arcade prevents).
- *(fix)* **Kiponus approach (site.js: "bridge/ramp to the upper city", unsourced): a single arcade (bay 18, piers 4) from the gate across the Tyropoeon to where the west slope regains the platform level (≈ 235 m), with an opening centred on the Tyropoeon street (its 14-amah openings clear the 13.3-amah street), deck 10 amot between 1-amah parapets** ⚙ — a single tier distinguishes it from the halachic double arcade of the Parah; a pier stood in the street before. — a stair down to the valley street.
- **Tadi: a short flight (7 half-steps) from the threshold down to the northern grade, 2 amot wider than the gate each side** ⚙.
