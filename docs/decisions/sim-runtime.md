# sim-runtime — decisions

Format: **Choice** — why (source / reasoning) — alternative.

## Runtime and engineering

- **Events map to modules by `event.choreo`. A module may serve several ids (`aliases`) and find its sub-events by event id (`sim.event(id)`, `sim.eventsOf(id)`).** — schedule.js groups several events under one choreo id (night-watch has 4, gates-opening has 4), and each needs its own window. — One module per event, which would duplicate actors and state.
- **Fixtures live inside each choreo module and are used only when the schedule has no event for that module. `ctx.schedule.events` stays empty while src/data/schedule.js is missing.** — The UI then falls back to its own richer fixtures, and the choreos can still be developed. — Exposing the sim's fixtures as schedule events, which would shrink the UI's sample list.
- **Data files are loaded with `import.meta.glob(['../data/schedule.js', '../data/zones-content.js'])`.** — The build passes while a parallel task has not yet written them. — Static imports, which break the build until both files exist.
- **cueAt uses a per-choreo crossing interval: the sim minutes accumulated from `time.frameDelta` since that choreo's last update.** — A choreo updated at 4 Hz (LOD) still catches every moment. A `time:jump`, `time:date`, negative speed or the first update after a choreo wakes clears the interval. Fired keys are cleared on jumps and expire after 720 sim minutes, so the next loop of the day fires them again. Verified with 271 of 271 greetings at 600×, no duplicates, and nothing fired on jumps. — Comparing the previous and current `t`, which mis-fires when the UI calls `time.set()` between frames.
- *(Superseded — see sim-runtime-fix.md.)* **A single figure-budget pass runs after all updates, capped at `quality.maxFigures`. The score is distance / (pool importance × choreo importance). Figures beyond `drawDistance / 5` are hidden.** — One global budget, with the officer and his torches (importance 3) kept over sleepers (1). people still draws LOD3 silhouettes under its own budget. — Fixed per-choreo quotas.
- **LOD update rates: under 120 m every frame, under 350 m at 15 Hz, beyond that at 4 Hz. After a jump every relevant choreo updates in the same frame.** — Distant scenes cost almost nothing, and determinism is unaffected because every pose is a function of t. — Per-actor update rates.
- **Idle micro-motion (breathing while standing or asleep) runs on real time, using people's automatic phase. Walking phases come from the distance walked, which is a pure function of t.** — At 600× a breathing cycle driven by sim time would flicker, and it carries no meaning. — Driving every phase from sim time.
- **Setters are change-cached in `Slot`, and floor lookups are cached by input plus `physics.version`. Multi-level spots resolve the floor with `physics.standFloor(x, z, hintY, exact = true)`.** — people's `setSite(X, Y, {near})` calls standFloor without `exact`, which prefers datum floors, so Beit Avtinas (+36) and the Chuldah passages (−27) would land on the datum floor (measured: +36 resolved to 16, −27 to 0). standFloor costs about 0.025 ms. — Relying on `setSite({near})`.
- *(Superseded — see sim-runtime-fix.md.)* **Walkers' routes are built once, asynchronously, from ctx.nav legs (one leg per macrotask). They are rebuilt when `physics.version` changes, or when nav becomes ready after a straight fallback. The wait loops on the current `nav.ready`.** — A collider rebuild replaces `nav.ready`, and the old promise settles without a grid (observed 5 s vs 10 s). — Straight lines, which walk through walls.
- *(Superseded — see sim-runtime-fix.md.)* **The officer's circuit passes through via points round the Gazit podium and its flight, west of the Azarah.** — `nav.findPath` returns null at once (lastExpanded 0) between the SW and NW corner posts and between the NW post and Lishkat HaParochet, yet succeeds leg by leg through (28, 381) and (20, 400) → (25, 430). This looks like a coarse-graph connectivity bug and has been reported to the player task. — Straight fallback legs.
- **Zones are tested at the feet + 0.4 amot (the UI's own offset), at 5 Hz, and immediately after `player:arrived`.** — The feet sit exactly on the floor plane of a zone box. — Testing the eye position.
- **Door amounts are pure functions of t (`doorAmount(t, tOpen, tClose, swing)`), and `open()` is called only when the value changes.** — The courts' and Heichal's `open()` recompute instanced bounding spheres. — Animating with tweens, which accumulate state.

## Research-driven (all minute offsets and spots are reconstruction ⚙)

- **The Levite guards hold no torches. Their posts are lit by the builders' night lamps and torches, and the torches go to the patrol: two bearers walk 2.3 m ahead of the Ish Har HaBayit.** — Middot 1:2 / Rambam BhB 8:10: "torches burning *before him*". Nothing gives the guards torches, and build-courts reached the same reading. — The brief's "guards with torches" (guard_levi's default prop), which is easy to restore in `guards: sim.pool({ … prop: null })`.
- *(Superseded — see sim-runtime-fix.md.)* **The officer visits the 20 reachable Levite posts. He does not visit the kohanic posts or the post behind the Beit HaKaporet.** — Only the Levites stand watch. The kohanim of the watch sleep (Tamid 1:1; Rambam BhB 8:6, "the kohanim who guard did not sleep in the priestly garments"), and the punishment is "the sound of a *ben Levi* being beaten" (Middot 1:2). The Kaporet post lies inside the Azarah, whose gates are locked at night. — Entering through the Moked wicket (Middot 1:7) to reach it.
- **"Shalom alecha!" is captioned as the officer's greeting at each post, naming the post.** — This is the Mishnah's plain reading and matches audio's 'shalom-call' caption. schedule.js gives the Rambam's reverse reading (the guard greets) in its description. — Captioning the Rambam's reading.
- *(Superseded — see sim-runtime-fix.md.)* **Circuit: Shushan → the Mount's NE, N, NW, W, SW corners and gates → both Chuldah passages → SE → the Azarah's south side → west → north → Nikanor. Pace 1.15 m/s, 21 s at each post, a round of about 40 min (about 14 rounds a night).** — The sources say only "he went round every post, all night". — Any other order.
- **The officer and the torch-bearers use role `guard_levi`.** — schedule.js: "the sources do not say whether he was a kohen or a Levite". The Rambam calls him one appointed over the watches. A staff prop is requested from people. — Role kohen.
- **No torches on Shabbat night (Friday after midday, and Shabbat before midday).** — By analogy with the dawn inspection, which carried no light on Shabbat nights (Rambam BhB 8:12). The posts' lamps were lit beforehand. The sleeping guard's garment is not burned on Shabbat night. — Torches every night.
- *(Superseded — see sim-runtime-fix.md.)* **The sleeping guard: the officer's clock is phased so he reaches the event's post (the nearest Levite post to `pos`, azarah-se) exactly at the event's start. That visit lasts the whole event, and later arrivals shift by the extra time.** — The sequence: "Shalom alecha" with no answer → blows (`throw` pose as a stand-in for a staff strike; a `strike` pose is requested) → the guard is up and steps aside → a torch-bearer lights the garment he slept under (a small fx fire with smoke) → it burns out. Captions quote Middot 1:2. — Teleporting the officer to the post.
- **The guard sleeps at his post, lying along the court's wall. That keeps him clear of the post's lamp stand, which build-courts set north or south of the post.** — Lying perpendicular to the wall put him through the stand. — Moving the sleeping spot.
- **Two young kohanim (rovim) asleep in each upper room, on the free floor clear of the worktable, jars and stair.** — Tamid 1:1 "the rovim guard there"; Rambam BhB 8:6 implies the kohanic guards slept. The count is not given. — Awake guards.
- **The Moked sleepers are staged here only while no `moked-night` module exists, in that event's window: 2 elders on the lowest tread of the rovadin, 3 young kohanim on the floor, and 1 kohen lying east–west over the keys slab. All are in the chol half.** — Tamid 1:1; Middot 1:8–9; no sleeping in the kodesh (Yoma 25a). The `lie` pose has its feet at the origin and its head opposite the heading. — Leaving the Moked empty until moked-night is written.
- **Guards' facing: at the Mount gates, into the gateway; at the Mount corners, over the Mount; at the posts of the courts and chambers, outward. They sway ±16° (seeded) and turn toward the officer within 12 m.** — A standing watch that looks at what it guards. — Fixed headings.
- **The locking comes from schedule.js `locking-up` (tzeit −15 → +5). The great gate and then the north wicket shut first, then the seven Azarah gates (0.20…0.41 of the window), then the five Mount gates at nightfall (0.75…), when the Levites are posted.** — Middot 1:9 ("the kohen locked from within"); Shekalim 5:1 (Ben Gever). — Closing everything at once.
- *(Superseded — see sim-runtime-fix.md.)* **The Mount and Azarah gates swing open with the three blasts (gates-blasts t0 + stagger). The great gate opens from within 0.3 min into `great-gate`: the inner pair first, then the outer, and the reverse at night. The north wicket opens when the clearing men reach it.** — Sukkah 5:5 "three for the opening of the gates"; Tamid 3:6–7; Middot 4:1 for the two pairs. — The gates open at different times.
- **The Ezrat Nashim east gate is not locked at night (left as the builder set it).** — No source places it in the locking, and the officer must reach the Nikanor post at the head of the 15 steps. — Locking it, which strands that post.
- **The trumpeters stand at the west end of Nikanor's passage (X = 255.8), not at schedule.js's X 258.** — At 258 the leaves, which swing into the passage, pass through them. — The data's spot.
- *(Superseded — see sim-runtime-fix.md.)* **The clearing men walk from the Gazit (the second lottery's view spot) up the Ulam steps to the north wicket, carrying the Teni (swapped for the keys at the wicket) and the Kuz, one prop per figure. One of them reappears, with the same seed, as the great gate's opener.** — Tamid 3:6–7. — Showing only the gate opening.
- *(Superseded — see sim-runtime-fix.md.)* **The nivreshet's flash is a caption only.** — A glint needs a render effect (requested). Gold under low sun may glint through bloom anyway. — A fake flame or light.
