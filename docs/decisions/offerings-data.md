# offerings-data — decisions (src/data/offerings.js, schedule.js, glossary.js, tools/coverage.mjs)

Format: **Choice** — why (source / reasoning) — alternative.

## Sources and method

- **Tractates outside ../sources read on Sefaria's API, Hebrew and English side by side** (Menachot, Nazir, Negaim, Keritot, Kinnim, Bikkurim, Bekhorot, Chullin, Temurah, Pesachim 57a–59b, Eruvin 32a, Ketubot 106a; Rambam Ma'aseh HaKorbanot, Temidin uMusafin, Pesulei HaMukdashin, Bikkurim) — the report's access layer; every step cites the halachah it rests on — the report's summaries alone (they do not cover individual offerings).
- **Sefaria's Mishnah numbering** (Zevachim 5:3 chatat, 5:4 olah, 5:5 ashamot, 5:6 todah and eil nazir, 5:7 shelamim, 5:8 bechor/ma'aser/pesach; 6:1 menachot, 6:2 the SW horn, 6:4–5 the birds; 9:6 limbs before midnight) — checked against the Bavli in ../sources — the "6:5 for birds" shorthand in the task brief (6:4 is the chatat ha'of, 6:5 the olat ha'of).
- **Shabbat 20a was checked and NOT used** for "no stirring on Friday night": its "zealous" reasoning is about the Pesach company, not the kohanim. The Friday-night rule rests on Rambam TuM 1:7 as the Kesef Mishneh reads it ("only on Friday" = by day, not Friday night, after Rava in Yoma 46a), with Pesachim 59b / Shabbat 24b ("not a weekday olah on Shabbat"). The plain halachah alone only says Friday's limbs are burned on Friday; "raised to the altar top but not on the fire" is the Ritva's (quoted in the Kesef Mishneh), not the Rambam's (fix after review).

## The individual offerings (offerings.js)

- **"Two that are four" at the NE then SW corner, below the red line; chatat on the four horns from the sovev, SE→NE→NW→SW; remainders on the southern yesod** — Zevachim 5:3–7; Tamid 4:1; Kinnim 1:1 ("olat behemah below"); Rambam MK 5:6–10 — the SE corner is never used for the two-that-are-four (no yesod there).
- **Lesser offerings staged at the rings too** — "anywhere in the court" (Zevachim 5:6–8) leaves the place open, and the rings are where an animal can be held; the model uses the eastern rows and keeps the two tamid rings clear — slaughter east or south of the altar (equally valid).
- **Six carriers for a lamb olah's limbs (+ flour + wine), eleven for a ram, twenty-four for a bull, for private olot as well** — Rambam MK 6:10–18 states it of every olah; Yoma 2:5–7's counts are for public offerings — one kohen per private olah (would contradict the Rambam).
- **Women do not lean or wave, except that a sotah and a nezirah wave their own; the yoledet stands at Nikanor** — Menachot 9:8; Rambam MK 9:16 ("a woman never waves except the sotah and the nezirah"); Sotah 1:5; Kelim 1:8 bars her from Ezrat Yisrael until the offering is made — showing her inside.
- **Firstling and tithe blood is POURED against the yesod (pose `pour`, gesture `shefichah`)** — Rambam MK 5:17 "בשפיכה"; the Mishnah says only "against the yesod" (Zevachim 5:8), Zevachim 37a weighs throwing against pouring, and Pesachim 5:6 says the Pesach's is "thrown, one throw"; the model follows the Rambam, as it does elsewhere — a throw (the earlier data, which had no source for it).
- **Chatat before olah, even a bird chatat before a lamb olah, cited to Zevachim 89a–90a (Mishnah 10:2, 10:4)** — the baraita "even a bird chatat before an animal olah" is on 90a; Rava there: the yoledet's olah is named first "for the reading" only — Pesachim 59b (cited earlier by mistake; it has nothing on this).
- **Melikah of the bird chatat is "one of the hard services", not "the hardest"** — Zevachim 64b rejects "the hardest" because kemitzah and chafinah are hard too.
- **The nazir's chatat takes no libations** — Menachot 9:6 exempts every chatat and asham except the metzora's — libations for all three (the earlier data).
- **The "kinnin" chest placed in Ezrat Nashim (station kinnin-chest)** — Shekalim 6:5 gives no place; Eruvin 32a has the yoledet drop her money in it, and Kelim 1:8 bars her from Ezrat Yisrael, so it must stand where she may — the report's 13 chests in the Azarah (would be out of her reach).
- **Tenufah east of the altar** — Rambam MK 9:6 ("before God, to the east", from Menachot 61a) — at the gate of Nikanor. The metzora's live asham and log are waved at Nikanor because he may not enter (marked derived).
- **Kitchen (cooking of the most-holy meat, the court's machavat/marcheshet and metal oven) in the SE of Ezrat Kohanim** — the sources put these inside the court (Zevachim 11:4; Rambam MK 12:22–23) but nowhere in particular; the SE corner keeps fires and pots away from the slaughter and the ramp traffic — the chavitin chamber (it is not clearly inside the hangings).
- **The burning place for notar = the ash place east of the ramp (altar.beit-hadeshen)** — Rambam MK 7:3 ("in the court, east of the ramp") and Zevachim 104b ("the great beit hadeshen in the court"); Tamid 1:4's ash place is also east of the ramp; whether they are one spot is unstated and said so — a separate fire pit.
- **Per-type frequencies are TOTALS that include the sets; sets carry `yields`; `alone` is derived** — the sim must not double-count (a yoledet's birds are inside chatat-haof's 20–60). One shared day-level quantile q makes `alone = total − Σ set·yields` exact and non-negative (the validator checks it); the yoledet's 15% lamb share and the metzora's 50% poor share are reconstruction. Nesachim are computed from their animals (13–49.5), not drawn — the earlier 10–35 was below its own drivers — independent per-type draws (would need the sim to reconcile).
- **Frequencies for an ordinary Cheshvan weekday are ranges, and birds dominate** — Shekalim 5:1 (an officer over bird pairs), 6:5 (two of thirteen chests for birds), Keritot 1:7 (the gold-dinar price of a pair), Eruvin 32a (birds offered daily from chest money); animal tithe clusters at the festivals (Bekhorot 9:5); meal-offerings are the poor's (Menachot 104b). No source gives daily counts; the ranges (≈20–70 animals, 40–120 birds) only need to be plausible and well under the lanes' capacity — single numbers (false precision).
- **Bikkurim: belongs in a 22 Cheshvan scene, but as an occasional sight** — 22 Cheshvan falls between Sukkot and Chanukah, when they are brought without the declaration (Bikkurim 1:6), and late choice olives or dates fit the season; schedule.js has no seasons, so its `bikkurim-late` event is reference-only ('occasional') while `offeringWindows(…, { hebrew })` opens a bikkurim lane in season (Shavuot–Chanukah, none before Shavuot — Bikkurim 1:3). The festival week itself is counted with the no-declaration season (the Mishnah's wording leaves it open) — a daily weekday event (would be wrong from Chanukah to Shavuot).
- **Bikkurim song kept, procession dropped** — Bikkurim 2:4 makes song a requirement of bikkurim (the Levites' "Aromimcha", 3:4); the ox and flute (3:3) belong to the ma'amad companies of the main season — no song (2:4 says otherwise).
- **No women among the bird-lane participants** — a bird takes no leaning (Rambam MK 3:7), so owners stay outside; women appear at Nikanor (nikanor-purifications) — unchanged from schedule-data.
- **Asset needs listed, not invented** — ASSET_NEEDS names the gestures (semichah, melikah, tenufah, kemitzah…), props (fork, hide, bird basket, loaf basket, pans, pots, razor) and animal variants (ewe, she-goat, kid, calf, cow, grown turtledove, young pigeon — Chullin 1:5) the steps want; `pose`/`prop` in the steps use only names ctx.people knows today.

## Day and night rules

- **Stream window: sunrise+106 (after the morning song) to 8.3 seasonal hours; blood by 8.35, fats and flaying by 8.4** — nothing before the morning tamid (Pesachim 58b), nothing slaughtered after the afternoon one (Pesachim 59a; Rambam TuM 1:3, which explains the 8½-hour tamid as leaving two hours for individuals) — starting right after the tamid's limbs (sunrise+88), during the libation and song.
- **Fats may go on until midnight; limbs are turned all night** — Rambam MK 4:2–3 (midnight, rabbinic), TuM 1:6 ("they turn them all night until morning", from Vayikra 6:2), Zevachim 9:6 (fallen limbs returned before midnight only). Shown as `evening-altar` (weekday, after the afternoon libation to midnight), `late-turning` (midnight to the civil date line) and `night-turning` (civil 00:00 to the clearing at alot−39) — "no attendants" all night (the earlier note), which the sources contradict.
- **One kohen "now and then" after midnight** — TuM 1:6 permits turning but no source says who did it or how often, and Tamid 1:3 (the dawn wicket from Beit HaMoked, "all is well") suggests the court stood shut; the event says so — a crew all night.
- **Friday: last fats before sunset; no tending on Friday night; Saturday night tended** — Rambam TuM 1:7 ("its beginning overrides Shabbat, its end does not") with the Kesef Mishneh and Yoma 46a (Rava), Pesachim 59b / Shabbat 24b ("not a weekday olah on Shabbat") — tending through Friday night.
- **Shabbat afternoon: the kohanim WORK at the pile** — Rambam MK 4:3: burning fats and limbs "overrides Shabbat in its time; they are not delayed until after Shabbat"; Yoma 46a (R' Meir: leftover limbs arranged "even on Shabbat"); the incoming mishmar offers the afternoon tamid (Sukkah 56b), so it is they who put its limbs on and tend them after Shabbat — standing by until sunset (the earlier note said "not stated", which MK 4:3 answers).
- **Night events split at the civil date line with `{ clock }` specs** — the schedule's day type belongs to the civil date, so a single midnight→alot event would show Friday-night tending on a Friday date (≈35 min) and lose Saturday night's. `late-turning` (weekday, shabbat) and `night-turning` (weekday, friday small hours) are exact. ctx.time.resolve already supports `{ clock }`; the validator and coverage resolvers gained it — one wrapping event with a known 35-minute error.
- **Leftovers (notar) burned in the morning, never at night** — Rambam Pesulei HaMukdashin 19:4–5 ("only by day"; not on Shabbat or a festival), Pesachim 7:10; so Friday's wait until Sunday. The user's "burning of the leftovers" at night is therefore shown as the altar's fats and limbs burning (which does go on at night), and the notar burning as a morning event — notar at night (contradicts the Rambam).
- **Evening kodashim meal until midnight, standing, also on Friday night** — Zevachim 5:3 ("that day and the night until midnight"), Rambam BhB 7:6 (no sitting in the Azarah); eating is permitted on Shabbat, cooking is not, so Friday night eats what was cooked before — weekday only.
- **Scouring the pots in the last 40 minutes before midnight, weekdays only** — Zevachim 11:4, 11:7 (in the court; before the eating time ends, against R' Tarfon); not on Friday night (heating water) — after midnight (the Sages require it before).
- **The kohanim of the evening are inside the locked court** — Middot 1:9 locks "from within"; the locking event's time is left as schedule-data set it (tzeit) — moving the locking to midnight (no source fixes either).

## Coverage (tools/coverage.mjs)

- **ACTIVE = participants > 0, except a LOW list (guards at posts, the watch in the upper rooms, sleepers in Beit HaMoked, gatekeepers, the ma'amad); NONE = no participants (the fire alone, the nivreshet flash)** — the brief's "static guards alone count as low" — tagging every event by hand in schedule.js (a format change).
- **Gaps are checked per day type (cyclic) and across civil midnight for the real week's transitions (weekday→weekday, Thu→Fri, Fri→Shabbat, Shabbat→Sun) on seven dates of the year; limit 10 min** — the brief's target. Result after this task: no gap on any date or day type (it was 25–47 min at dusk and after nightfall). The Ish Har HaBayit's torch-lit rounds are what an observer sees in the Shabbat small hours; that is the sources' Friday night.
